# AMB — 2Q2026 / 1H2026 Results Summary

- **Company:** Allianz Malaysia Berhad (AMB, Bursa ticker 1163)
- **Reporting date:** Six months ended 30 June 2026
- **Report status:** Unaudited
- **Source file:** `2026 Q2 AMB KLSE.pdf`
- **Archived:** 2026-10-14

## Executive summary
Insurance revenue grew 7.0% YoY to RM3.261b. Profit before tax rose 4.8% to RM595.5m and profit after tax rose 4.4% to RM446.0m. Life insurance drove profit growth, while general insurance pre-tax profit fell 5.0% as claims and expense ratios increased. Investment income rose sharply, partly due to gains on FVTPL investments, so this component should be treated as market-sensitive rather than automatically recurring.

## Segment data (1H2026 YoY)
- General insurance revenue RM1.844b (+5.5%); pre-tax profit RM298.2m (-5.0%).
- Life insurance revenue RM1.417b (+9.0%); pre-tax profit RM294.1m (+14.4%).
- Investment holding pre-tax profit RM3.2m versus a loss of RM3.1m in 1H2025.

## Operating indicators
- General insurance claims ratio: 60.4% vs 55.4%; expense ratio: 25.3% vs 24.2%; combined ratio: 87.6% vs 86.9%.
- Life insurance gross written premium: RM2.364b (+16.0%); ANP RM487.6m (+8.7%); expense ratio 10.8% vs 11.4%; persistency ratio 81.5% vs 84.8%.

## Legal update
On 18 August 2026, the Court of Appeal dismissed MyCC's leave appeal in the case related to general-insurance trade discounts and labour rates. AGIC's portion of costs was RM5,000, according to the report.

## Management outlook
Management cited Malaysia's 2Q2026 economic growth of 6.0%. Priorities include strengthening distribution, productivity, underwriting and claims management, digital initiatives and customer experience. No profit forecast or profit guarantee was issued.

## Investment interpretation
**Positive:** Revenue growth across both segments; life-insurance profitability; higher equity; no borrowings/debt securities disclosed.
**Caution:** General-insurance claims ratio deteriorated; life persistency weakened; profit growth trails revenue growth; investment income is market-sensitive.
**Next checks:** 3Q2026 results, claims/combined ratio, persistency, recurring investment returns, capital adequacy, current share price, P/E, P/B and dividend yield.
