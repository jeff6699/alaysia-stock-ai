# Bursa Malaysia Source Map V1.8

## Source tiers
Use the tiers below, then apply research/evidence/source_priority.md for exact conflicts and period-specific precedence. Preserve all sources and explain selected-source decisions; never silently replace.

### Tier 1 — official / primary
1. Bursa Malaysia official disclosures and filings.
2. Company annual reports.
3. Company quarterly reports and interim reports.
4. Official investor presentations.
5. Official company announcements and disclosures.

### Tier 2 — reputable secondary
- Reputable financial databases (record vendor methodology, timestamps and underlying-source notes).
- Broker research (identify author, date, estimate versus reported data, assumptions and any paywall limits).
- Industry research (identify publisher, definition, coverage and methodology).

### Tier 3 — secondary / lead only
- Financial news.
- Other secondary sources, including unattributed or unverified material.

Tier 3 material is not sole verification for a material company fact, financial metric, valuation input or investment conclusion. Use it as a lead and seek an authoritative source. Record a conflict when it differs from an official source.

## Source-to-data map
| Information type | Preferred source | Capture details |
|---|---|---|
| Announcements, corporate transactions, financial results | Bursa official disclosure and the attached issuer filing | Announcement ID/title, issuer, filing/publication date, event date, reporting period, document/page/note |
| Annual financial statements | Issuer annual report filed with Bursa / official company report | Fiscal year, audited status, consolidated/parent basis, statement and note/page |
| Quarterly/interim results | Bursa filing / issuer interim report | Quarter and YTD periods, review status, comparable period, currency/unit, statement/note |
| Investor presentations | Official issuer presentation | Presentation date, reporting period, slide/page, forward-looking statement versus historical fact |
| Company profile and segment detail | Annual report, official site/disclosure | Exact document/date and the claim's scope |
| Share price, volume and market fields | Dated Bursa observation or reputable data provider with disclosed basis | Trading date/time, adjusted basis, share class, currency, volume unit, provider |
| Dividends | Bursa/company announcement and report | Proposed/declared/approved/ex/payment dates, DPS, currency, share basis |
| Macro data | Responsible official agency/regulator; then reputable database | Series, geography, reference period, publication/revision date, unit |
| Industry data | Official statistical/regulatory source or transparent industry research | Definition, date, geography, method and scope |

## Search and attribution
Keep issuer identity and security class explicit. Distinguish Bursa filing date from the period or event date. An investor presentation is a primary source for what the issuer presented, not independent evidence that its outlook will be achieved. Database values retain provider/source notes and are cross-checked for period, units, restatements and share basis.

For every collected file or page, create source metadata as described in research/evidence/source_metadata.md. If a document or exact locator is inaccessible, set verification status REQUIRES_VERIFICATION and do not upgrade it by inference.

## Missing source handling
If a source or document is unavailable, state N/A or REQUIRES_VERIFICATION with search/access limits. No secondary source may be silently substituted for an unavailable official filing.
