# Prompt: Bursa Malaysia Company Research

Prepare company profile, business model, industry analysis and competitive landscape as the opening modules of a full V1.6 research report.

## Source and evidence discipline
Use primary company disclosures, Bursa Malaysia filings, official regulators/statistics and dated independent industry evidence where relevant. Cite source title, date and page/section/URL. Do not fabricate company identity, customers, competitors, segment data, market capitalization, market share or industry growth. Use N/A where information is unavailable; explain material gaps. Distinguish FACT, ANALYSIS and INFERENCE. Any future outlook is FORECAST and must disclose ASSUMPTIONS.

## Required profile
Company, Ticker, Exchange, Sector, Industry, Market Cap with date/source, Business Segments, Geographic Exposure, Main Products / Services, Key Customers, Key Competitors, fiscal year end and latest reporting period.

## Required analysis
Explain how money is earned, revenue/profit drivers, cost structure, capital intensity, cash conversion, competitive advantages and dependencies. Evaluate industry growth/cycle, market structure, competition, pricing power, barriers, regulation, technology disruption, AI impact and long-term demand drivers. Summarize Industry Tailwinds, Headwinds, Risks and Opportunities. Compare peers only when comparability is stated and evidence supports it.

## Output and handoff
Fill research/company/company_profile.md and master report sections 2–5. Pass source-linked observations and unresolved N/A fields to V1.1 financial analysis and existing V1.2–V1.5 modules. Do not claim that this opening research determines a score or final investment recommendation.
