# Prompt: Bursa Malaysia Company Research

## Objective
Prepare a balanced, sourced investment research report for a Bursa Malaysia listed company from supplied filings and reliable primary sources. Never fabricate financial data, citations, market share, peers, management guidance, risks, catalysts, valuation inputs or forecasts.

## Inputs
- Issuer name and Bursa code: [Provide]
- Research date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
- Latest quarterly/interim and annual reports: [Provide]
- Bursa announcements and primary sources: [Provide]
- Share price/date/source if valuation is requested: [Provide]
- Currency/unit and research horizon: [Provide]

If issuer, source or input is missing, state “Data unavailable” and its effect; do not guess. Record consolidation basis, period, filing date, units, audit/review status and restatements. Cite material facts by filing, page/note and date.

## Procedure
1. Establish like-period comparisons (quarter vs comparable quarter, YTD vs YTD, audited FY vs FY).
2. Reconstruct revenue, gross profit/margin, operating profit, PBT, net/attributable profit, basic/diluted EPS, ROE and ROA when supported.
3. Analyze balance sheet, receivables, contract assets/liabilities, inventory, debt, leases, liquidity and commitments.
4. Reconcile OCF, define FCF, separate cash capex from additions, and review dividends.
5. Analyze earnings quality, adjusted items, working capital, estimates, related parties and audit observations.
6. Review reported segments, competitive position and moat with sourced evidence; do not invent market shares.
7. Assess management commentary, risks, catalysts, corporate events and forward outlook. Separate announced from completed and confirmed from speculative.
8. Value only with a dated share price, sourced inputs, method, assumptions and sensitivity; prepare Bull/Base/Bear cases or state unavailable.
9. Check current Bursa notices for PN/GN status only if relevant, citing current primary notice.
10. Adapt ratios to sector; explain where conventional industrial-company ratios are unsuitable.

## Evidence labels
In every substantive section keep separate:
- FACT — source-backed or transparently calculated, cited with period and unit.
- ANALYSIS — comparison, explanation, ratio, accounting or valuation method.
- INFERENCE — qualified judgment with evidence, uncertainty and alternatives.
- FORECAST — forward estimate/scenario with horizon, assumptions, method and sensitivity.
Write “Data unavailable” when unsupported. Never blend forecast with reported fact.

## Required report structure
Produce all sections in this order:
1. Executive Summary
2. Latest Results
3. Financial Performance
4. Balance Sheet
5. Cash Flow
6. Earnings Quality
7. Business Segments
8. Competitive Position
9. Moat
10. Risks
11. Catalysts
12. Valuation
13. Bull Case
14. Base Case
15. Bear Case
16. Investment Conclusion

Use the four evidence labels in each section. Include source list, calculations, research limitations, explicit data gaps, and a concise disclaimer that the work is informational, not investment advice or a buy/sell/hold recommendation.
