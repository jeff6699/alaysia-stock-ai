# Prompt: Full Malaysian Stock Research Report — V1.6

Prepare an evidence-led, end-to-end research report for a Bursa Malaysia listed company using the repository's V1.6 pipeline and the unchanged V1.2, V1.3, V1.4 and V1.5 modules.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Never fabricate company identity, financial data, customers, competitors, risks, catalysts, prices, forecasts or citations. Use N/A and explain how it affects the report.
- Identify the latest available financial period and its filing status. Prefer a five-year history when available; say explicitly when less is available.
- Cite each material fact with source, date and page/note/URL locator. Distinguish issuer statements from independently verified outcomes.
- Label FACT, ANALYSIS, INFERENCE and FORECAST separately; label every analyst-selected valuation/scenario input ASSUMPTION.
- Do not alter V1.2 quality rules, V1.3 AI moat rules, V1.4 valuation formulas, or V1.5 decision/risk/catalyst rules. Use their versioned outputs and audit trail.
- Bear/Base/Bull scenario probabilities must sum exactly to 100% before a standard final classification. If not supportable, explain N/A and follow V1.5 gates.
- Do not add code or dependencies; produce the modular Markdown report.

## Workflow
Follow research/research_pipeline_v1.md in order:
1. Company identification.
2. Business model.
3. Industry analysis.
4. Competitive landscape.
5. Financial statement analysis.
6. Earnings quality.
7. Cash flow analysis.
8. V1.2 Investment Quality Score.
9. V1.3 AI Moat Evolution.
10. V1.4 Valuation.
11. V1.5 Risk Engine.
12. V1.5 Catalyst Engine.
13. Bull/Base/Bear scenarios.
14. V1.5 Investment Decision Engine.
15. Final Investment Thesis.
16. Monitoring checklist.

## Required company profile
Capture Company, Ticker, Exchange, Sector, Industry, Market Cap, Business Segments, Geographic Exposure, Main Products / Services, Key Customers and Key Competitors. Unknown fields are N/A; do not infer identities.

## Financial and contextual analysis
Analyze revenue, growth, gross profit/margin, operating profit/margin, net profit, EPS, ROE, ROIC, debt, cash, free cash flow, capex, operating cash flow, dividend and dilution/buybacks. Cover growth, profitability, balance sheet, cash flow, capital allocation and earnings quality. Explain formulas and comparability. Evaluate industry cycle, structure, competition, pricing, entry barriers, regulation, disruption, AI impact and long-term drivers, with tailwinds/headwinds/risks/opportunities.

## Integrated scoring and valuation
Report original V1.2 Quality Score/Rating/strengths/weaknesses; V1.3 Current Moat, Durability, 3-Year Evolution, AI Disruption Risk and AI Opportunity; V1.4 Current Price, Bear/Base/Bull values, MOS, confidence and separately labeled assumptions. Show V1.5 risks ranked by impact/probability/time horizon and catalysts by near/medium/long timeframe. Trace every output to its source module and date.

## Required final report
Use research/company/full_research_report_template.md exactly, including all 20 sections. Include scenario Revenue, Margin, EPS, FCF, Valuation, Fair Value, Expected Return, assumptions and main risk; thesis reasons to win/fail, what must go right and invalidators; final V1.5 score, rating and why; monitoring table with positive and negative signals; data confidence; key evidence; disclaimer.

Complete reports/report_quality_checklist.md before treating the report as publishable.
