# Prompt: Financial Statement Analysis

Analyze the supplied Bursa Malaysia quarterly/interim/annual reports as a careful equity research analyst. Use only supplied or verifiable source data; never invent a value, citation, driver, guidance or period.

## Inputs
- Issuer and Bursa code: [Provide]
- Period(s): [Provide]
- Consolidated or parent-only basis: [Specify]
- Currency and unit: [Provide or identify from filing]
- Bursa announcements, reports, excerpts or URLs: [Provide]
- Research question: [Optional]

If a required input is missing, state “Data unavailable” and identify its effect. Confirm reporting period, filing date, units, basis, audited/reviewed/unaudited status, amendments, and comparable periods. Cite every material fact by filing, date, page/note or URL.

## Analyze where disclosed
1. Revenue and comparable growth.
2. Gross profit and gross margin.
3. Operating profit, PBT, tax and net profit; separate profit attributable to owners and non-controlling interests.
4. Basic/diluted EPS, weighted-average shares and dilution.
5. ROE and ROA with formulas and average-balance convention.
6. Borrowings, leases, cash, net debt and stated leverage definitions.
7. OCF and profit-to-cash reconciliation.
8. FCF with explicit definition/exclusions; capex cash payments versus asset additions.
9. Receivables, contract assets, ageing, collection/ECL; inventory, write-downs and inventory days when calculable.
10. Dividend status, dates, per-share amount, cash paid and payout basis.
11. Segment revenue/profit/mix and reconciliation.
12. Earnings quality, management-adjusted earnings, unusual items and accruals.
13. Management commentary, risks, catalysts and forward outlook.

Compare quarter with comparable prior-year quarter, YTD with prior-year YTD, and audited years separately. Adapt to business model; explain if industrial-company ratios mislead for banks, insurers, REITs, developers, contractors, plantations or others.

## Output
For every material topic provide separate headings:
### FACT
[Source-backed figures, periods, units, locator and formula.]
### ANALYSIS
[Comparable trend, mix, drivers and reconciliation.]
### INFERENCE
[Qualified implication, alternative explanations and uncertainty.]
### FORECAST
[Forward scenario only with horizon, assumptions, method and sensitivity; otherwise state “No forecast made; data or assumptions are insufficient.”]

Finish with findings, unavailable inputs, follow-up questions and limitations. Do not allege misconduct from a ratio or red flag. Distinguish management guidance from analyst estimates. Do not describe proposed dividends or planned projects as paid/completed. Do not calculate EPS from period-end shares where weighted-average shares apply.
