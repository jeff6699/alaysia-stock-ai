# Prompt: Earnings Quality Analysis

Assess persistence and cash backing of reported earnings for a Bursa Malaysia listed company using provided primary filings. A signal is a diligence question, not proof of manipulation or misconduct. Do not invent numbers, citations, explanations or missing data.

## Inputs and setup
- Issuer / Bursa code: [Provide]
- Periods: [Quarter, comparable quarter, YTD, audited FY]
- Sector: [Identify from source or say unavailable]
- Bursa filings / interim and annual reports: [Provide]
- Optional source data: [Provide]

Record units, currency, consolidated/parent basis, period lengths, restatements and audit/review status. Cite each material fact by source, date and page/note. Write “Data unavailable” where an input is missing.

## Review
1. Reconcile profit to OCF; identify non-cash adjustments, tax/interest classification and working capital.
2. Compare earnings-to-cash conversion over matched periods using a stated numerator and denominator.
3. Review revenue, receivables, contract assets/liabilities, ageing, ECL, collections and concentration.
4. Review inventory, WIP, write-downs/reversals, cost recognition, and inventory days only when calculable.
5. Examine fair value, disposals, impairment/reversals, provisions, FX, tax items and other unusual items.
6. Reconcile management’s core/adjusted profit to reported profit; test recurring exclusions and treatment of gains/losses.
7. Review capitalized development/PPE, depreciation, impairment assumptions and cash capex versus additions.
8. Review related-party balances/transactions, estimates, contingencies, tax, auditor opinion, key audit matters and going-concern statements.
9. Compare management commentary/guidance with prior statements and outcomes. Do not turn qualitative optimism into a numerical forecast.
10. Adapt to sector and explain inapplicable ratios.

## Required format
For every material finding separate:
### FACT
[Values, calculation, source/date/page/note, period and unit.]
### ANALYSIS
[Reconciliation, trend, accounting context and sector interpretation.]
### INFERENCE
[Calibrated implication, uncertainty, benign alternatives and follow-up.]
### FORECAST
[Assumption-based scenario with horizon and sensitivity, or “No forecast made; data or assumptions are insufficient.”]

Conclude with stronger / mixed / weaker support for earnings or insufficient evidence; summarize the evidence, missing inputs and follow-up questions. Do not make allegations without evidence. Keep the four labels distinct throughout.
