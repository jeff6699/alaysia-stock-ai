# Prompt: Malaysian Company Financial Analysis

Analyze the supplied Bursa Malaysia company filings using research/company/financial_analysis.md and the repository's V1.1 financial statement, earnings-quality and cash-flow frameworks.

## Rules
- Use source data only; no fabricated figures, ratios or adjusted metrics.
- Prefer five annual periods plus the latest available interim/quarterly result. Identify exact period, filing date, audited/reviewed/unaudited status, consolidation basis, currency, units and share basis.
- If any input is unavailable, write N/A, identify source gap, and explain effect. Do not substitute zero or an unsupported peer estimate.
- Cite each reported value and calculation input by report name, date and page/note/URL locator.
- Keep FACT, ANALYSIS, INFERENCE and FORECAST distinct. Label forward estimates FORECAST and all selected inputs ASSUMPTION.
- Adapt ratios to the company's industry and explain where standard industrial metrics are not meaningful.

## Coverage
Assess revenue and growth, gross profit/margin, operating profit/margin, net profit, EPS, ROE, ROIC, debt, cash, operating cash flow, free cash flow and its definition, capex, dividends, share dilution/buybacks, working capital, receivables, inventory, segments, capital allocation and earnings quality. Discuss trends, one-offs, balance-sheet resilience, cash conversion, risks and outlook. Reconcile statements and disclose restatements or corporate actions where relevant.

## Output
Fill research/company/financial_analysis.md; summarize Financial Analysis, Earnings Quality and Cash Flow in sections 6–8 of research/company/full_research_report_template.md. Do not calculate or alter V1.2 scoring rules; pass evidence-based inputs to scoring/investment_scorecard_v1.md and record its published output separately.
