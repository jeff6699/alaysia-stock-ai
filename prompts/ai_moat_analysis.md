# Prompt: AI Moat Evolution Analysis

## Role
Assess a Bursa Malaysia company’s competitive moat and its likely evolution over the next three years, including AI disruption and AI-enabled opportunities. Use evidence supplied or verified from primary/credible sources. Never invent evidence, customer retention, market share, AI use, economics, patents, risks or management actions.

## Inputs
Company / Bursa ticker: [Provide]  
Industry / business segments: [Provide]  
Analysis date: [YYYY-MM-DD]  
Current filings and 3-year history: [Provide]  
Peer / customer / supplier / industry evidence: [Provide]  
AI announcements, deployments and outcomes: [Provide]  
Other primary sources: [Provide]

If evidence is missing, mark relevant fields N/A and explain effect on score and confidence. Mark a moat dimension N/R only with a business-model reason. Lack of disclosure is not proof that the moat is absent; if relevant evidence is unavailable, use N/A. Weak or absent evidenced moat is scored low.

## Required dimensions
Score relevant dimensions 0–10 using scoring/ai_moat_scoring_rules.md:
1. Brand Power
2. Cost Advantage
3. Scale Advantage
4. Switching Costs
5. Network Effects
6. Distribution Advantage
7. Technology / IP
8. Data Advantage
9. AI Capability
10. Capital / Ecosystem Advantage

For every dimension document what it measures, evidence and sources, score rationale, weak signals, warning signals, strength signals, how it may improve, and how it may deteriorate. Avoid assuming all dimensions apply.

## Evidence labels
Clearly separate:
- FACT — source-backed fact/statement, date, page/URL and period.
- ANALYSIS — comparisons and causal assessment.
- INFERENCE — qualified judgment, uncertainty and alternative explanations.
- FORECAST — explicit Year +1, +2 and +3 expectations, assumptions and falsifiers.

Do not present a management claim as independently verified outcome. Do not convert an announcement into proof of execution. Do not fabricate financial, customer, IP or AI evidence.

## 3-year assessment
For every relevant dimension state:
- Year 0/current strength and evidence.
- Year +1 expected direction and prerequisites.
- Year +2 expected direction and prerequisites.
- Year +3 expected direction and prerequisites.
- Net 3-year trajectory: +2 strongly strengthening, +1 strengthening, 0 stable, −1 weakening, −2 rapidly deteriorating; justify code and note confidence.
Explain whether expected path is delivered, merely planned, or speculative.

## Composite
Calculate:
- Current Moat Score: relevant dimension points normalized to 0–100 with applicable/evidenced coverage.
- Moat Durability: five-factor rubric /20.
- 3-Year Evolution Score: mean relevant dimension trajectory mapped with 5 × (mean + 2), max 20.
- AI Disruption Risk: residual Low / Moderate / High / Unknown, plus inverse-risk resilience points /15 (15 means low risk).
- AI Opportunity Score: incremental moat-enhancement potential /15, excluding double counting current AI Capability.
- Overall AI Moat Evolution Score: weighted components of 30 + 20 + 20 + 15 + 15 = 100, using scoring/ai_moat_evolution.md. N/A components reduce available points; show coverage. Do not assign zero to N/A or hide gaps by normalization.
Apply the final rating rules and mark provisional/insufficient evidence when coverage is below requirements.
