# Source Priority and Conflict Resolution V1.7

## Financial statement source hierarchy
For a specific issuer financial figure, use this priority order when sources conflict, while checking scope and filing status:

1. Bursa Malaysia filing / official company filing for the exact announcement or statement.
2. Annual report.
3. Quarterly/interim report.
4. Investor presentation.
5. Broker research.
6. Financial media.
7. Unverified sources.

Company announcements and official company disclosures are primary sources. Prefer the filing that specifically reports the relevant period/metric; an annual report may be more complete, while a later interim report is more current for its period. This ordering does not mean an older annual report overrides a newer quarterly filing for current-quarter facts. Exact scope, period, restatement, audited/reviewed status and subsequent corrections govern selection.

## Other evidence
For market data, use the dated Bursa observation or a reputable provider with clear methodology; identify differences in adjusted price, timing, volume or share basis. For macroeconomic data, prefer the responsible official statistical agency/regulator or official series owner. For industry context, prefer dated official data and transparent reputable industry research. Secondary evidence may corroborate or surface conflicts but does not silently replace an issuer/regulator filing.

## Conflict procedure
1. Create separate evidence records for each materially conflicting value/claim.
2. Compare issuer, metric definition, period, unit, currency, consolidation basis, shares basis, publication date, audit/review status and restatement history.
3. Check later Bursa/company correction or restatement. Preserve superseded values and link the superseding record.
4. Apply the hierarchy above to the exact question; state the chosen source and reason.
5. If conflict persists, label evidence Contradicted, mark the affected data MISSING/N/A for decision use, and lower confidence. Do not average conflicting financial statements.
6. Disclose consequential conflict and any effect on V1.2–V1.6 outputs; never silently change their methodology.

## Source-quality reminder
A/B/C is source quality; Strong/Moderate/Weak/Contradicted/N/A is evidence strength for a claim. A primary disclosure is strong evidence that the issuer made the disclosure, but not proof of a future target. A weak secondary source is a lead to verify, not sole support for a material conclusion.

## Evidence labels and missing data
- FACT: directly source-supported observation or reproducible calculation with its inputs and formula.
- ANALYSIS: interpretation of verified facts with method and limitations.
- INFERENCE: qualified conclusion derived from evidence, including uncertainty and alternatives.
- FORECAST: conditional forward view with horizon and explicitly identified ASSUMPTIONS.
Missing evidence is MISSING and displayed as N/A with its downstream effect; do not fabricate or silently substitute a value.
