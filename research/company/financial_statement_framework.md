# Financial Statement Analysis Framework

## Purpose and evidence rules
Use this modular framework to analyze Bursa Malaysia quarterly, interim, and annual reports. Start from filed statements and notes; preserve consolidation basis, reporting period, currency, units, filing date, audit/review status, source locator, and any restatement. Prefer Bursa Malaysia announcements and issuer filings; use presentations only as supplemental sources.

Keep four labels separate for every material topic:
- FACT — source-backed statement or transparent calculation, with period, unit, source/page/note, and formula.
- ANALYSIS — comparison, reconciliation, ratio, or accounting interpretation.
- INFERENCE — qualified conclusion beyond what the source states; give evidence, uncertainty, and alternatives.
- FORECAST — forward estimate only, with horizon, assumptions, method, and sensitivities.

If a needed datum is absent, write “Data unavailable” and state which analysis it limits. Never fabricate figures, drivers, guidance, or citations.

## Period and reconstruction protocol
1. Identify issuer, Bursa code, financial year end, period end, filing date, currency/unit, consolidated or company-only basis, and audited/reviewed/unaudited status.
2. Build separate columns for latest quarter, comparable prior-year quarter, latest YTD, prior-year YTD, latest audited FY, and prior audited FY. Do not mix standalone quarters with YTD or full-year values.
3. Record amendments/restatements, period-length differences, segment boundary changes, accounting policy changes, acquisitions/disposals, and share-capital changes.
4. Read relevant notes for revenue recognition, receivables/contract assets, inventory, impairment, leases, debt, tax, provisions, related parties, segments, subsequent events, and commitments.
5. Keep reported amounts distinct from analyst calculations and management-adjusted measures. Use the issuer’s stated units; show any normalization or conversion.

## Income statement and returns
For each of revenue, gross profit/margin, operating profit, PBT, tax, net profit and attributable profit, report FACT / ANALYSIS / INFERENCE / FORECAST separately.

- Revenue: compare like periods; discuss price, volume, mix, FX, acquisitions/disposals, customer concentration, contract assets/liabilities, and related-party revenue only with evidence. Revenue growth is not proof of collection or durable demand.
- Gross margin = gross profit / revenue. Explain mix, prices, input costs, utilization, FX, and classification only where evidenced.
- Operating profit: preserve the reported label; distinguish it from PBT, adjusted, or “core” profit. Reconcile material other income/expense, FX, fair value, impairment, and disposal items.
- Net profit: separate PBT, tax, profit for period, owners’ profit, and non-controlling interests. Explain tax credits, incentives, losses, and prior-year tax adjustments. Label the denominator when using an effective tax rate.
- EPS: record reported basic/diluted EPS and weighted-average shares. Explain placements, rights issues, bonus issues, splits, options, convertibles, and dilution. Do not calculate EPS with period-end shares where weighted-average shares are required.
- ROE = profit attributable to owners / average equity attributable to owners. ROA = profit for period / average total assets. State the period and annualization convention; use average balances where available, otherwise label the proxy. Interpret with leverage, acquisitions, impairment, equity changes, seasonality, and sector.

## Balance sheet and working capital
- Debt/leverage: reconstruct short- and long-term borrowings, leases, cash, restricted cash, and obligations from notes. State definitions: net debt = borrowings (show lease treatment) less cash and cash equivalents; debt/equity = defined debt / defined equity. Review maturities, rates, FX, security, covenants and refinancing only as disclosed. Do not claim covenant compliance without evidence.
- Receivables: separate trade, contract assets, other, related-party balances, and ECL allowance. Receivable days = average trade receivables / revenue × days in period. State period length and balance definition; review ageing, overdue amounts, concentration, write-offs, disputes and collections.
- Inventory: record classes, write-downs/reversals, ageing, obsolescence, work in progress, and pledged stock where disclosed. Inventory days = average inventory / cost of sales × days in period, only when inputs are comparable. Explain seasonality and mix.
For all three topics use separate FACT / ANALYSIS / INFERENCE / FORECAST blocks and mark missing notes explicitly.

## Cash flow, capex, and dividend
- Operating cash flow: reproduce the filed figure and bridge from profit through non-cash adjustments, receivables, contract balances, inventory, payables, provisions, tax, and interest as classified by the issuer. Explain timing versus recurring effects only where supported.
- Free cash flow: define the convention. A common measure is net operating cash flow minus cash purchases of PPE and intangible assets. Show reported inputs, sign convention, exclusions, and analyst result. Acquisitions, development costs, lease principal and other investing outflows must not be silently mixed in.
- Capital expenditure: distinguish cash paid from additions accrued/capitalized in notes; reconcile capital creditors, lease additions, acquisitions, disposals and construction in progress where possible. Separate maintenance/growth capex only with evidence or a labeled estimate.
- Dividend: distinguish proposed, declared, approved, ex-date, payment date, and cash paid; show per-share and total amounts. Payout ratio = dividends attributable to period / profit attributable to owners, with timing and special distributions explained. Assess funding alongside cash generation, capex, borrowing and liquidity.

## Segments, management commentary and outlook
Reproduce reported segments and measures before analysis. Compare revenue, segment profit/margin, assets, capex and geography when disclosed; reconcile eliminations and unallocated items. Do not create missing segment results. Analyze concentration and stated drivers.
Cite management statements and separate direct commentary (FACT) from analyst evaluation (ANALYSIS), implications (INFERENCE), and quantified analyst estimates (FORECAST). Distinguish guidance, targets, aspirations and general optimism; compare with prior statements and outcomes where available.

## Bursa Malaysia and sector adaptation
Check relevant current Bursa filings/notices, filing status, material corporate exercises, related-party transactions, share issues, dividends, litigation and subsequent events. Confirm any current PN/GN status from current Bursa notices; do not rely on stale claims.
Adapt ratios to business model: banks/insurers need sector capital, funding and asset-quality measures; REITs need distribution and gearing context; property developers need launches, sales, collections and project commitments; contractors need order book, claims and contract assets/liabilities; plantation firms need crop, yield, commodity and biological-asset context. Use only relevant, disclosed data. State when industrial-company FCF or leverage metrics mislead.

## Output and completion checks
End each material topic with the four evidence labels in order, even if a label says no forecast made.
- [ ] Periods, units, consolidation basis and audit/review status are clear.
- [ ] Revenue, gross margin, operating/net profit, EPS, ROE and ROA covered.
- [ ] Debt, OCF, FCF, receivables, inventory, capex, dividends and segments covered.
- [ ] Earnings quality, management commentary, risks, catalysts and outlook addressed or marked unavailable.
- [ ] FACT, ANALYSIS, INFERENCE and FORECAST never blended.
- [ ] Calculations state formula, inputs and sources; no unsupported values are inserted.
