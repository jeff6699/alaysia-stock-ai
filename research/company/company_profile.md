## Business model
### How the company makes money
Describe products/services, who pays, payment basis, contract/transaction structure, revenue recognition timing when relevant, and recurring vs project/commodity exposure.

### Revenue drivers
Separate volume, price, mix, capacity/utilization, subscriptions/renewals, contracts/order book, commodity/foreign exchange or other drivers as applicable.

### Profit drivers and cost structure
Explain evidenced factors affecting gross profit, operating costs, financing and taxes; distinguish recurring items from one-offs.

### Capital intensity and cash conversion
Describe working capital, fixed-asset requirements, maintenance/growth investment only where supported, payment terms, capex cycle and cash conversion. Link to financial_analysis.md.

### Competitive advantages and dependencies
Summarize sourced advantages and dependencies: customers, suppliers, licenses, commodity inputs, skilled labor, technology, financing, channel, government contracts or key individuals. Do not call an advantage a durable moat without V1.3 evidence.
