## Trend analysis
### Growth and profitability
Explain revenue and profit growth, gross/operating margins, product/geographic mix, seasonality and cycle effects. State formulas, periods and any comparability changes.

### Balance sheet
Review cash, borrowings, maturities, net debt/cash, interest cover, lease commitments, liquidity, working capital and contingent obligations where disclosed. Use sector-appropriate measures; do not apply industrial ratios blindly to financial institutions.

### Cash flow and capital allocation
Link operating cash flow, working-capital movements, capex, FCF convention, dividends, buybacks, share issuance and acquisitions. State whether distributions are funded by recurring cash generation when evidence permits.

### Returns
ROE and ROIC formulas must state numerator, average/closing denominator, tax/lease treatment and period. Explain one-offs and sector-specific limitations. N/A if source detail does not permit a comparable calculation.

### Earnings quality
Complete a separate analysis in research/company/earnings_quality_framework.md and summarize the conclusion here. Address accruals, one-offs, provisions, receivables, inventory, contract balances, capitalized costs, related parties and cash conversion when disclosed.

## Evidence labels
For each material metric or conclusion, use:
- FACT — reported figure, period, units, source/locator or transparent calculation inputs.
- ANALYSIS — trend, peer/sector comparison, reconciliation and caveats.
- INFERENCE — qualified implication, uncertainty and alternative explanation.
- FORECAST — only a separately labeled estimate/scenario with all ASSUMPTIONS and horizon.
Maintain an audit trail for every formula. No fabricated numbers.
