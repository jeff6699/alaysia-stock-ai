# CIMB Group Holdings Berhad (1023) — 2Q26 Press Release Archive

> Source: CIMB Group Holdings Berhad, “CIMB records resilient 2Q26 with RM1.94 billion net profit and 11.2% ROE”, released 28 August 2026.
> Scope: issuer press release only. This note preserves management-reported figures and outlook; it does not replace the interim financial statements.

## 1. Executive summary

CIMB reported a resilient 2Q26, with quarterly net profit of **RM1.94bn**, up **1.2% QoQ**, and annualised ROE of **11.2%**. The result was supported by broad-based business performance, stronger non-interest income, loan/asset growth and lower operating expenses.

For 1H26, the company maintained a **55% payout ratio** and declared a first interim cash dividend of **19.65 sen/share**, representing a total payout of about **RM2.1bn**.

## 3. What drove the result

### Positive
- **NOII +6.0% QoQ** to RM1.83bn, driven by higher franchise fees and sustained client sales momentum.
- **NII +1.3% QoQ** to RM3.73bn.
- Assets grew **2.2% QoQ** and loans **1.1% QoQ** on a constant-currency basis.
- Deposits grew **2.5% QoQ** on a constant-currency basis.
- Operating expenses declined **1.6% QoQ**, improving CIR by 200bps to **45.2%**.
- GIL ratio reached an **all-time low of 1.6%** as at June 2026.
- Capital remained strong, with **CET1 at 14.0%**.

### Margin issue
Management acknowledged continued NIM pressure, but expects it to progressively ease, supported by **loan repricing in Indonesia** and continued growth in **lower-cost deposits**.

## 4. Strategy / Forward30

CIMB is targeting structural ASEAN opportunities, especially:
- AI and data centres
- Wealth
- Cross-border trade
- Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ)
- ASEAN Financial Passport
- China-ASEAN partnership
- ASEAN Corporate Hub in Singapore
- Transaction Banking and regional roll-out of CIMB OCTO Biz

CIMB is also reallocating capital away from underperforming businesses and scaling its digital proposition through the combined strengths of the universal bank and TNG Group.

**TNG Group profit grew more than 100% YoY in 1H26.**

## 5. Digital finance milestone

CIMB completed Malaysia's first pilot of **tokenised deposits** to subscribe for a tokenised sukuk.

- RM1.38bn raised from 12 institutional investors
- Part of a RM1.68bn sukuk issuance

Management views this as evidence of the potential for digital-asset innovation to improve financial processes and Malaysia's financial infrastructure.

## 6. Management outlook

Management expects:
- Continued asset and loan growth supported by a healthy pipeline.
- Further operational efficiency and stronger execution.
- Disciplined capital allocation.
- Continued multi-year capital returns.
- Greater scale from the digital proposition and TNG Group.
- Long-term growth opportunities from ASEAN structural shifts and AI.

## 7. Investor interpretation

**Overall reading: POSITIVE / RESILIENT, but not yet a high-growth result.**

The most important positives are:
1. Sequential earnings improvement.
2. Stronger fee/non-interest income.
3. Better cost efficiency.
4. Improving asset quality.
5. Strong capital and dividend capacity.
6. Clear ASEAN/digital growth strategy.

The main item to verify against the full financial statements is whether **NIM pressure and higher credit costs** are still suppressing 1H earnings growth. The press release itself emphasises progressive NIM easing as a forward catalyst.
