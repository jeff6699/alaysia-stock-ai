# CIMB Group Holdings Berhad (1023) — 2Q26 / 1H26 Research Summary

> Source basis: CIMB Group Holdings Berhad unaudited condensed interim financial statements for the period ended 30 June 2026. This note is a research-layer summary; it does not replace the original filing.

## 1. Executive conclusion

**Status: FACT + ANALYSIS**

CIMB's 1H26 result is best characterised as **resilient earnings with margin pressure, higher credit costs, but improving operating efficiency and stable asset quality**.

- 1H26 attributable net profit: **RM3.855bn**, versus RM3.862bn in 1H25 (**-0.2% YoY**).
- 1H26 PBT: **RM5.158bn**, versus RM5.275bn (**-2.2% YoY**).
- Operating income: **RM10.972bn**, versus RM11.101bn (**-1.2% YoY**).
- NII: **RM5.372bn**, versus RM5.605bn (**-4.2% YoY**).
- Other non-interest income: **RM1.996bn**, versus RM1.819bn (**+9.8% YoY**).
- Operating expenses: **RM5.070bn**, versus RM5.131bn (**-1.2% YoY**).
- Loan ECL: **RM798m**, versus RM696m (**+14.7% YoY**).
- 1H annualised ROE: **11.0%**.
- Gross impaired loan ratio: **1.64%**, improved from **1.72%** at 31 Dec 2025.

The key debate for future valuation is therefore not balance-sheet survival, but **whether NIM stabilisation/recovery and fee/wealth growth can offset provision normalisation and restore earnings growth**.

## 2. Balance sheet

**FACT**

At 30 June 2026:
- Total assets: RM794.85bn, +2.1% vs 31 Dec 2025.
- Gross loans/advances/financing: RM458.08bn, +1.1%.
- Customer deposits: RM483.73bn, +1.3%.
- Total equity: RM71.68bn, -0.3%.
- Net assets per share: RM6.50 vs RM6.52.

Loan growth is positive but moderate. Deposit growth broadly supports balance-sheet expansion.

## 3. Earnings quality

**ANALYSIS**

The earnings mix is shifting away from pure spread income:
1. NII declined 4.2% YoY.
2. Other non-interest income increased 9.8% YoY, supported by wealth, treasury client sales and other income according to management.
3. Costs declined 1.2% YoY, indicating continued cost discipline.
4. Higher ECL prevented the operating improvement from translating into PBT growth.

This makes **NIM + credit cost** the two most important financial variables to monitor in the next two quarters.

## 4. 2Q26 momentum

**FACT**

2Q26 attributable net profit was RM1.938bn, +2.6% YoY and +1.2% QoQ. Operating income was RM5.562bn, +2.8% QoQ.

Management reported:
- NII +1.3% QoQ.
- NOII +6.0% QoQ.
- Operating expenses -1.6% QoQ.
- Cost-to-income ratio improved to 45.2%.
- 2Q26 annualised ROE 11.2%.

This indicates that sequential momentum improved even though the 1H YoY picture remained broadly flat.

## 5. Business segments

**FACT / ANALYSIS**

1H26 segment net income:
- Consumer Banking: RM4.443bn.
- Commercial Banking: RM2.048bn.
- Wholesale Banking: RM3.094bn.
- CIMB Digital Assets & Group Funding: RM1.387bn.

Management's 2Q26 commentary:
- Consumer Banking PBT declined YoY due to higher provisions and lower NIM.
- Commercial Banking PBT declined YoY in 2Q26, while 1H26 performance was supported by provision writebacks in Indonesia and lower expenses.
- Wholesale Banking was the strongest 2Q contributor, with PBT growth supported by stronger NOII and increased writebacks.
- Digital Assets & Group Funding was affected by transformation-related expenses, while TNG Digital continued strong growth.

## 6. Asset quality and risk

**FACT**

Gross credit-impaired loans were RM7.522bn at 30 June 2026, with a gross impaired loan ratio of 1.64%, versus 1.72% at end-2025.

Geographic gross impaired loans:
- Malaysia RM3.919bn
- Indonesia RM1.724bn
- Thailand RM1.127bn
- Singapore RM231m

The filing also shows 1H26 loan ECL of RM798m versus RM696m in 1H25.

**ANALYSIS:** asset quality is currently stable/improving by GIL ratio, but the increase in ECL means credit costs remain an earnings headwind.

## 7. Capital and shareholder return

**FACT**

At 30 June 2026:
- CET1 before proposed dividend: 14.902%.
- CET1 after proposed dividend: 14.325%.
- Total capital ratio before proposed dividend: 18.611%.
- Total capital ratio after proposed dividend: 18.034%.
- Proposed 1H26 interim dividend: **19.65 sen/share**.
- Stated payout ratio: **55%**.
- Proposed payout: approximately **RM2.124bn**.

Capital remains a major strength and provides capacity for growth and dividends.

## 8. Strategic catalysts

**FACT / MANAGEMENT OUTLOOK**

The filing highlights:
- ASEAN cross-border trade and connectivity.
- Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone.
- ASEAN Financial Passport.
- China-ASEAN partnership.
- ASEAN Corporate Hub in Singapore.
- Transaction Banking and CIMB OCTO Biz.
- AI and data-centre opportunities.
- Wealth management.
- Digital assets/tokenised deposits.

CIMB also completed Malaysia's first pilot of tokenised deposits for a tokenised sukuk subscription, raising RM1.38bn from 12 institutional investors as part of a RM1.68bn sukuk issuance.

## 9. Material risk item

**FACT**

CIMB Investment Bank is named as one of 12 defendants in litigation relating to the MEX II Sukuk Murabahah. Plaintiffs claim losses and damages and allege joint and several liability including a claimed “Dissolution Amount” of RM1.38bn as at 3 Jan 2022. CIMB Investment filed its defence and later defended a counterclaim. Trial dates are scheduled in December 2027, April 2028, May 2028 and June 2028.

**ANALYSIS:** this is a material legal-risk item to monitor, but the filing does not establish that CIMB will ultimately pay the claimed amount.

## 10. Investment tracking conclusion

**Not a valuation or buy/sell decision yet.**

Current research stance:
- **Business quality:** Strong.
- **Balance sheet:** Strong.
- **Asset quality:** Stable/improving.
- **Capital:** Strong.
- **Dividend:** Positive.
- **Earnings growth:** Neutral; 1H26 broadly flat.
- **Main near-term issue:** NIM and credit costs.
- **Main upside trigger:** NIM recovery + loan/deposit growth + sustained NOII/wealth/treasury growth + cost discipline.
- **Main downside trigger:** prolonged NIM compression, higher ECL, weaker ASEAN macro conditions, or adverse litigation development.
