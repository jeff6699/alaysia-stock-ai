# NationGate Holdings Berhad (NHB) — 2Q26 / 1H26 Financial Report Summary

> Source: Unaudited Interim Financial Report for the Second Quarter Ended 30 June 2026. This research note is based only on the uploaded filing.

## 1. Executive conclusion

**2Q26 shows a clear sequential recovery, but 1H26 remains materially weaker YoY.**

- 2Q26 revenue: **RM1.769b**, down about **32% YoY**, but up **2.8% QoQ**.
- 2Q26 gross profit: **RM58.4m**, down about **21% YoY**, but up **106.5% QoQ**.
- 2Q26 PBT: **RM8.1m**, versus RM83.3m a year ago; however, 1Q26 was a RM8.4m loss, so the company returned to profit sequentially.
- 1H26 revenue: **RM3.489b**, down **35.2% YoY**; PBT was **RM0.37m loss** versus RM157.8m profit in 1H25.
- The main weakness is **Data Computing**, while Networking & Telecommunications and Automotive are showing stronger contributions.
- Balance-sheet risk has increased: borrowings reached **RM901.9m**, cash was **RM321.1m**, and gearing increased to **0.88x** from 0.70x at end-2025.
- Operating cash flow remained negative at **RM97.9m** for 1H26, with receivables increasing substantially.
- Management expects continued opportunities from **AI, cloud computing, digital infrastructure and photonics**, with photonics-related facilities expected to be completed in **4Q2026**.

## 4. Segment picture

### Data Computing — main weakness
1H26 revenue was **RM3.024b**, representing roughly **86.7% of group revenue**. It fell from RM5.079b in 1H25, a decline of about **40.5%**.

### Networking & Telecommunications — recovery
1H26 revenue was **RM274.9m**, up about **7.7% YoY**. In 2Q26, revenue was RM164.6m versus RM134.3m, up about **22.5% YoY**.

### Automotive — strongest growth
1H26 revenue was **RM96.9m**, versus RM24.9m, an increase of about **289% YoY**. 2Q26 revenue was RM47.4m versus RM12.9m.

### Other segments
Industrial instruments remained relatively stable, while Consumer Electronics increased from a small base.

## 5. Geographic mix

Malaysia remained the largest market at **RM2.958b** in 1H26. USA revenue rose sharply to **RM166.8m** from RM27.5m a year earlier. Other Asian countries also increased to RM228.1m from RM43.8m.

Singapore declined materially to RM28.1m from RM605.4m in 1H25.

## 6. Balance sheet and financial risk

At 30 June 2026:

- Cash: **RM321.1m**
- Borrowings: **RM901.9m**
- Net debt: approximately **RM580.9m**
- Gearing: **0.88x**, versus 0.70x at 31 December 2025
- Trade receivables: **RM1.522b**
- Trade payables: **RM1.131b**
- Inventory: **RM684.1m**
- PP&E: **RM450.2m**

The key issue is that leverage increased while earnings weakened. Receivables are also large relative to the earnings base, so cash conversion should be monitored closely.

Borrowings consist mainly of secured bankers' acceptances (**RM710.4m**), term loans (**RM147.7m**), revolving credit (**RM34.0m**) and finance leases (**RM9.8m**).

## 7. Cash flow

1H26 operating cash flow was **negative RM97.9m**.

Major movements included:
- Inventory released **RM461.1m** of cash.
- Receivables consumed **RM743.5m**.
- Payables contributed **RM144.6m**.
- Capex was **RM93.8m**.
- Dividends paid were **RM33.9m**.
- Financing activities provided **RM89.8m**.

Cash fell from RM417.5m at end-2025 to RM321.1m at 30 June 2026.

## 8. Investment thesis — source-derived

### Potential positives
1. **AI / cloud / digital infrastructure exposure:** management explicitly expects continued demand for server-related manufacturing and advanced technology solutions.
2. **Photonics:** the group is advancing photonics initiatives, with related facilities expected to be completed in 4Q2026.
3. **Segment diversification:** Networking & Telecommunications and Automotive are growing and partially offsetting Data Computing weakness.
4. **USA exposure:** US revenue increased materially in 1H26.
5. **Sequential recovery:** 2Q26 returned to positive PBT after a loss in 1Q26.

### Key risks
1. **Data Computing concentration:** the segment still represents the vast majority of revenue and has fallen sharply.
2. **Profitability compression:** 1H26 PBT is slightly negative and PAT is only RM3.3m.
3. **Negative operating cash flow:** cash generation has not yet recovered.
4. **Higher leverage:** gearing increased to 0.88x.
5. **Receivables:** RM1.52b trade receivables require close monitoring.
6. **FX exposure:** the group uses USD-denominated purchases and had RM2.238b of FX forward contracts, equivalent to USD548m.
7. **Execution/capex risk:** approved and contracted PP&E commitments were RM362.8m.

## 9. Catalysts to monitor

- Recovery of Data Computing orders.
- Continued growth in Networking & Telecommunications.
- Sustained Automotive growth.
- Completion and ramp-up of photonics facilities in **4Q2026**.
- Improvement in operating cash flow and receivables collection.
- Stabilisation/reduction of gearing.

## 11. Dividend

The filing records FY2026 interim dividends of **0.25 sen/share** paid on 29 June 2026 and another **0.25 sen/share** proposed on 21 August 2026 and paid on 22 September 2026. FY2025 also included a 0.25 sen interim dividend and 1.00 sen special dividend.

## 12. Preliminary investment view

**Status: WATCH / RECOVERY CASE — not yet a clean earnings-growth case.**

The most important question is not whether NHB has AI exposure; the filing already indicates that opportunity. The key investment question is whether the current AI/data-centre-related demand can translate into **sustainable revenue recovery, margin expansion and positive operating cash flow** while leverage remains controlled.

**Next research stage:** valuation → AI/competitive moat → risks → catalysts → Bull/Base/Bear → final investment decision.

> Note: No valuation, target price, stop-loss or buy/sell rating is assigned here because this filing alone does not provide the market price, valuation multiples or sufficient forward earnings information.
