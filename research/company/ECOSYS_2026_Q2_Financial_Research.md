## 1. Executive Summary

ECOSYS delivered a profitable first reported interim period, with Q2 FY2026 revenue of RM30.99m and net profit of RM3.12m. 1H FY2026 revenue reached RM53.54m and net profit RM5.36m.

The main investment highlights are:

1. **RM94.33m outstanding purchase orders** as at 25 August 2026.
2. **Semiconductor exposure** through UHP and Abatement businesses.
3. **India growth opportunity**, with FY2025 India revenue of RM38.22m, representing 35.1% of Group revenue.
4. **RM39.34m IPO proceeds** planned for capacity expansion, debt repayment, operational capability, India expansion and working capital.
5. **New Simpang Ampat factory** intended to increase space and production capacity.

The key concern is cash conversion: 1H FY2026 operating cash flow was **-RM6.41m**, while inventory and receivables were elevated.

### Core investment test

> **Orders → Revenue → Profit → Cash Flow → Long-term Value**

The RM94.33m order book is encouraging, but the investment thesis becomes stronger only if ECOSYS can convert orders into sustainable revenue, earnings and free cash flow.

## 3. Business Segments

### UHP — Ultra-High Purity
- Q2 revenue: **RM17.74m**
- Group contribution: **57.2%**
- Provides precision engineering components and sub-assembly modules used in ultra-high-purity fluid processing solutions for the semiconductor industry.

### Abatement
- Q2 revenue: **RM13.25m**
- Group contribution: **42.8%**
- Covers research, development, customisation, assembly, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance of abatement systems for the semiconductor industry.

Both segments provide exposure to semiconductor manufacturing capacity expansion.

## 4. Order Book

As at **25 August 2026**, outstanding purchase orders were **RM94.33m**.

- FYE2026 delivery: **RM63.42m**
- FYE2027 delivery: **RM30.91m**

The order book provides meaningful near-term revenue visibility and is larger than 1H FY2026 revenue.

### Interpretation

A large order book is positive, but it is **not the same as recognised revenue**. Key execution questions are:
- Can orders be delivered on time?
- Can they be recognised as revenue?
- Will margins remain attractive?
- Will the orders generate positive operating cash flow?

## 5. India Expansion

India is already a meaningful market for ECOSYS.

- FY2025 India revenue: **RM38.22m**

### Investment implication

India could become a long-term growth engine if semiconductor manufacturing investment continues to expand and ECOSYS successfully converts new customers into recurring business.

## 11. Final Initial View

**Status: WATCHLIST**

ECOSYS has a compelling semiconductor-related growth story supported by a RM94.33m order book, India exposure and IPO-funded capacity expansion.

However, the company should not be judged on orders alone.

The most important transition to monitor is:

> **Order Book → Revenue → Profit → Operating Cash Flow → Free Cash Flow**

If this conversion is demonstrated over the next several quarters, the investment thesis can strengthen materially. If profit grows while cash flow remains weak, the working-capital risk becomes more important.

**Conclusion:** Worth watching, but valuation and cash-flow conversion matter.
