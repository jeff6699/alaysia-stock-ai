# Company Risk and Catalyst Framework

## Purpose and evidence labels
Use this checklist to document company-specific risks, potential catalysts, management commentary and forward outlook for Bursa Malaysia issuers. A signal is a diligence question, not proof of failure or wrongdoing.

For every material claim keep separate:
- FACT — source-backed event/data, date, period, unit and filing page/note.
- ANALYSIS — exposure, transmission channel, possible effect, mitigation and timing.
- INFERENCE — qualified judgment, evidence, uncertainty and alternatives.
- FORECAST — quantified scenario only with horizon, assumptions, method and sensitivities.

If evidence is missing, state “Data unavailable.” Do not invent likelihoods, impact amounts, guidance or probabilities. Prefer current Bursa announcements, interim/annual reports, issuer filings and relevant regulator/primary sources. Mark issuer presentations as management-provided.

## Catalysts
Classify each as confirmed, planned, contingent or speculative. Record the announcement/source/date, milestone and status. Separate FACT (what is announced or completed), ANALYSIS (causal path and dependencies), INFERENCE (credibility and uncertainty), and FORECAST (estimated effect only with assumptions). Include observable confirmation and failure conditions. An aspiration is not a confirmed catalyst.

## Management commentary and outlook
Capture exact or faithful paraphrase of statements on demand, orders, pricing, input costs, utilization, capacity, capex, financing, currency, strategy, regulation and outlook. Cite source/date/page. Distinguish quantified guidance, target, plan, aspiration and general commentary. Compare with previous guidance and reported outcomes when available. Do not convert qualitative optimism into numeric forecasts.

## Bursa Malaysia and local context
As relevant, verify stock code, material announcements, corporate exercises, substantial shareholding changes, related-party transactions, dividends, litigation and listing status from current primary filings. Check current Bursa notices for any Practice Note (PN) or Guidance Note (GN) status and state the date checked; do not rely on stale news or infer status. Identify Malaysian regulatory, licensing, tax, labor, land, environmental and sector-policy exposure only where applicable and sourced. Do not present a notice or dispute as a finding of wrongdoing.

## Scenario and monitoring
Bull, Base and Bear cases should begin with sourced FACT, explain driver mechanics in ANALYSIS, state uncertainty/alternatives in INFERENCE, and put assumptions/ranges/horizon/sensitivity under FORECAST. State when quantification is unavailable. List leading indicators, sourced upcoming milestones and triggers to revisit the view. Do not provide unsupported target prices or probabilities.
