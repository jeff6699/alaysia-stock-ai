## Evidence convention
Use four clearly separate labels in every section:
- FACT — sourced or transparently calculated; cite source, page/note, period and units.
- ANALYSIS — comparison, explanation, ratio, accounting or valuation method.
- INFERENCE — qualified judgment beyond source statements, with uncertainty and alternatives.
- FORECAST — forward view with horizon, assumptions, method and sensitivity.
If a datum is absent, write “Data unavailable” and explain the limitation. Never fabricate financial data. For each section use separate labeled blocks, or a table with those four labels as distinct columns.
