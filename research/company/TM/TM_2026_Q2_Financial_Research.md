# TM 2Q 2026 / 1H 2026 Financial Research

**Company:** Telekom Malaysia Berhad (TM)  
**Announcement:** 20 August 2026  
**Reporting period:** Quarter and six months ended 30 June 2026  
**Source:** Company news release — TM 2Q 2026 Results Announcement

## Executive summary
TM reported continued underlying growth. 2Q26 revenue rose 6.8% YoY to RM2.96bn and underlying PATAMI rose 4.6% to RM406.3m. 1H26 revenue rose 4.8% to RM5.90bn and underlying PATAMI rose 7.0% to RM842.6m.

Reported PATAMI was RM365.5m in 2Q26 and RM687.0m in 1H26 after Prihatin, foreign-exchange and other stated one-off adjustments.

## 1H26 business segments
- **B2C / Unifi:** RM2.91bn, +5.2% YoY; fixed broadband base 3.23m.
- **B2B / TM One & Credence:** RM1.36bn, +1.9% YoY.
- **C2C / TM Global:** RM1.55bn, +6.8% YoY; >9,500 5G backhaul sites supporting >80% network coverage.

## CAPEX and growth priorities
1H26 CAPEX was RM555.7m, 9.4% of revenue. Management expects CAPEX to accelerate in 2H26. Focus areas are data centres, cloud, GPUaaS and 5G access.

Key developments: IPDC Block 2 fully taken up; Asia Link Cable landed in Sedili; TM Nxera development; expansion of 5G backhaul, submarine/cross-border connectivity and GPUaaS.

## Earnings quality
Underlying PATAMI growth (+7.0%) exceeded revenue growth (+4.8%) in 1H26. However, reported PATAMI was materially below underlying PATAMI because of stated adjustments and one-off costs. Future valuation should therefore separate recurring earnings from these items.

## Dividend
Second interim dividend: 7.0 sen/share. Total 1H26 dividend: 13.5 sen/share, stated by TM as 75% of reported PATAMI.

## Investment-research implications
- All three customer segments grew; TM Global was the fastest-growing segment.
- Data centre, cloud and GPUaaS are explicit future growth areas.
- 2H26 CAPEX acceleration is a key execution variable.
- Dividend support remains positive, but sustainability should be tested against normalized earnings and free cash flow.
- One-off adjustments must be tracked to ensure earnings quality improves rather than repeatedly relying on normalization.
