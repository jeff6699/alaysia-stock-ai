> This record distinguishes reported figures from investment interpretation. Figures are in RM unless otherwise stated.

## 1. Executive summary

Malakoff's 1H FY2026 results weakened materially year-on-year, mainly because lower energy and capacity payments from Tanjung Bin Power (TBP) were affected by the Unit 30 steam-turbine rotor failure and Unit 20 generator hydrogen-cooler leakage. Revenue fell 18.9%, profit before tax fell 32.2%, and profit attributable to the company's equity holders fell 46.5%.

Q2 improved sequentially: profit before tax rose to RM70.6m from RM26.6m in Q1, helped by better fuel margins at TBP and Tanjung Bin Energy (TBE) and higher contributions from associates. This is an improvement from Q1, but it does not erase the year-on-year decline.

**Initial view:** operational recovery and debt/cash-flow monitoring are the key investment questions. The report alone is insufficient to determine whether the share is undervalued; a current share price, updated earnings outlook and valuation comparison are needed.

## 3. Why performance fell

Management's stated explanation:
- Lower capacity payments from TBP due to the Unit 30 steam-turbine generator rotor failure and Unit 20 generator hydrogen-cooler leakage.
- Higher energy payments from Segari Energy Ventures (SEV) and Prai Power (PPSB), supported by a higher despatch factor, partly offset the weakness.
- Insurance-claim receivables and stronger associate contributions also partly offset the decline.

Other income was RM200.7m in 1H FY2026 versus RM16.4m a year earlier. This helped cushion the operating decline, so investors should examine the nature and repeatability of other income rather than treating it automatically as recurring earnings.

Sequentially, Q2 PBT was RM70.6m versus Q1's RM26.6m, according to management, due mainly to improved fuel margins at TBP and TBE and higher associate contributions.

## 6. Dividends and funding

- The company paid a final dividend of **0.86 sen per ordinary share** on 29 May 2026 for FY2025, totalling approximately RM42.0m.
- A RM450m Islamic Medium-Term Notes issuance by Malakoff Power Berhad was disclosed; proceeds were intended to finance eligible projects under its sustainable-finance frameworks.
- Scheduled repayments disclosed: TBE sukuk RM55m, TBOM sukuk RM60m and Malakoff trade financing RM245m.
- The report does not establish the future dividend amount or guarantee a continuation of the same payout.

## 7. Outlook and catalysts stated in the report

- New PPAs were executed on 18 May 2026 with TNB to extend the GB3 and SEV gas-fired plants in Lumut until 2029. Management expects this to support recurring income and electricity supply security.
- A 5 June 2026 MoU with TNB REMACO covers power-plant maintenance, repair and overhaul services, training and competency development.
- The Tanjung Bin Complex coal-handling system was reported fully restored to design capacity of up to 5,000 tonnes per hour. At the report date, TBP Unit 30 rotor repair was progressing, with reinstallation expected in mid-August 2026. This was a forecast in the report, not confirmation that the repair was completed.
- Management described itself as prudent and cautiously optimistic for FY2026.
- Malaysia GDP growth of 6.0% in Q2 2026 versus 5.4% in Q1 was cited by the company as macroeconomic context; stronger GDP alone does not guarantee improved results for Malakoff.

## 8. Principal risks to track

1. **Plant availability / equipment risk:** duration and financial impact of TBP Unit 30 and Unit 20 issues; subsequent operating availability and insurance recovery.
2. **Earnings quality:** unusually large increase in other income; confirm recurring versus non-recurring components in subsequent reports.
3. **Leverage and interest:** borrowings of RM7.64bn and 1H interest paid of RM174.3m.
4. **Cash conversion:** 1H operating cash flow dropped to RM116.8m; receivables rose substantially and cash balances declined.
5. **Capital intensity:** 1H property, plant and equipment purchases were RM261.8m and concession-asset purchases RM83.5m; unprovided capital commitments totalled RM1.03bn.
6. **Legal contingency:** the report discusses a long-running SIAC arbitration concerning the Prai Power Plant and GE entities. The supplied excerpt does not show the final outcome; review the full latest disclosure before assessing exposure.
7. **Dividend sustainability:** avoid extrapolating the FY2025 final dividend without updated earnings and cash-flow evidence.

## 9. Investment interpretation (not a recommendation)

**Potential positives**
- Q2 PBT recovered sequentially from Q1.
- Associates and joint ventures contributed RM86.2m of share of profit in 1H, versus RM48.0m a year earlier.
- PPA extensions to 2029 may help provide contracted operating visibility.
- Waste management/environmental services and technical services offer business diversification beyond power generation.

**Negatives / evidence still needed**
- 1H revenue, PBT, attributable profit and EPS all declined materially year-on-year.
- Gross profit fell faster than revenue, indicating margin pressure.
- Operating cash flow weakened sharply while cash fell and borrowings increased.
- No share price, market capitalisation, forward earnings estimate or valuation multiple is contained in this report; therefore no defensible target price, valuation conclusion or buy/sell call can be made from this document alone.

**Provisional stance from this report only:** Watch / wait for evidence of sustained operational recovery and stronger cash flow. Reassess after checking the latest quarterly report, plant-repair status, insurance receipts, current share price and dividend declaration.
