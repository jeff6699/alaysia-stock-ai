## Investment profile

Northern Solar is primarily a solar PV EPCC business. As of Q1 FY2027, EPCC remained overwhelmingly the main revenue contributor. The group's stated growth direction includes utility-scale solar, expansion of solar assets and opportunities arising from LSS6 and BESS.

## Key financial observations

1. Revenue growth was strong, driven mainly by higher solar PV EPCC revenue.
2. Gross profit declined despite higher revenue because the project mix contained a higher contribution from lower-margin projects.
3. PBT and PAT increased only marginally year-on-year.
4. Q1 FY2027 versus Q4 FY2026: revenue +84.1%, gross profit +25.9%, PBT +11.2%, PAT +27.8%.
5. Trade and other receivables increased to RM51.91m from RM34.31m at 31 March 2026.
6. Operating cash flow was only RM0.19m, mainly affected by the RM17.25m increase in receivables.
7. Borrowings declined to RM9.60m from RM10.32m at 31 March 2026.

## Growth catalysts

- Malaysia's renewable-energy transition and NETR.
- LSS6: 2,500MW solar generation quota paired with 1,250MW BESS capacity.
- Potential participation in LSS6 bidding, independently or through strategic partnerships.
- Expansion of utility-scale solar projects and solar assets.
- New EPCC contracts and operating-efficiency improvements.

## Risks / watch items

- Project gross-margin compression.
- Receivables growth and weak operating cash conversion.
- Dependence on EPCC revenue.
- Execution and project-mix risk.
- LSS6 participation does not guarantee contract wins.

## Investment interpretation

**Current assessment:** Growth thesis remains intact, but Q1 FY2027 does not yet demonstrate operating-leverage improvement. The most important validation points for subsequent quarters are gross-margin recovery, conversion of receivables into cash, new EPCC order intake, and progress on LSS6/BESS opportunities.

**Important:** Facts and interpretations should remain clearly separated. This record is a research archive, not a buy/sell instruction.
