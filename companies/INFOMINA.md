## 1. Executive summary

Infomina reported strong year-on-year growth in Q1 FY2027: revenue rose 39.6% to RM76.19m, profit before tax rose 27.3% to RM12.56m, and profit after tax rose 26.8% to RM10.30m. Growth was mainly driven by technology infrastructure operations, maintenance and support services. The company disclosed an outstanding order book of RM614.2m and a new regional Broadcom mainframe software distribution/support appointment covering 12 Asia-Pacific markets.

The key quality-of-earnings caution is cash conversion: operating cash flow was negative RM24.70m, largely reflecting a RM48.33m working-capital outflow from trade and other receivables and a RM7.97m increase in contract assets. Cash and cash equivalents fell to RM75.12m from RM94.02m year-on-year (cash-flow statement basis). Revenue growth is encouraging, but collection and working-capital management need monitoring.

## 4. Balance sheet and cash flow

- Cash, deposits and bank balances on the statement of financial position: RM85.93m (31 Aug 2026), down from RM124.02m at 31 May 2026.
- Cash and cash equivalents on the cash-flow statement basis: RM75.12m at quarter end, versus RM94.02m at 31 Aug 2025.
- Operating cash flow: **negative RM24.70m**, versus positive RM2.15m in Q1 FY2026.
- Investing cash flow: negative RM7.73m, including RM4.98m additions of intangible assets.
- Financing cash flow: negative RM9.04m, including RM4.08m dividend paid and RM4.65m share buy-back.
- Total borrowings: RM22.70m, comprising RM19.03m revolving credit, RM1.75m hire-purchase payables and RM1.93m lease liabilities.
- Trade receivables rose to RM145.67m from RM97.36m at 31 May 2026; contract assets rose to RM22.47m from RM14.48m.
- Current liabilities were RM239.51m and current assets RM378.41m; current ratio approximately 1.58x.
- Net assets per share: 27.29 sen, versus 26.66 sen at 31 May 2026.

**Interpretation:** reported profit growth has not translated into positive operating cash flow this quarter. Receivables and contract assets, billing/collection timing and the sustainability of cash generation should be key follow-up checks. The cash-flow statement cash balance and statement-of-financial-position cash/deposit balance are different measures; do not treat them as interchangeable.

## 5. Order book, strategy and prospects (company disclosure)

- Outstanding order book increased to **RM614.2m**, following contracts in Malaysia involving the Road Transport Department (JPJ) and a Malaysian financial institution, and a contract in Japan from Panasonic Digital Co., Ltd.
- Completed acquisition of 51% equity interest in Blue Fortress Sdn Bhd, expected by the company to complement cybersecurity, digital transformation and managed technology services.
- Appointed by Broadcom to distribute and support its Mainframe Software portfolio across 12 Asia-Pacific markets, including four new markets: Australia, New Zealand, South Korea and India. The company said it had been named Broadcom Software Mainframe Partner of the Year for five consecutive years from 2021 to 2025.
- The board remained optimistic barring unforeseen circumstances, while acknowledging global economic uncertainties.
- No dividend was recommended for the current quarter. The report records a 0.68 sen second interim dividend for FY2026 paid on 19 August 2026.

**Analytical note:** the order book offers potential revenue visibility, but timing of conversion, delivery capacity, margin profile and cash collection are not disclosed in sufficient detail here to estimate future earnings precisely. Broadcom distribution rights expand market access but should not be assumed to generate material profits without segment/order disclosures.

## 6. Risks and monitoring checklist

1. **Cash conversion / receivables:** track operating cash flow, receivable days, overdue balances and contract asset conversion in subsequent quarters.
2. **Margin pressure:** gross margin eased from approximately 27.3% to 26.4%; PBT margin also fell year-on-year.
3. **Customer and contract concentration:** the filing names several major contracts but does not give customer concentration percentages.
4. **Execution risk:** order book conversion depends on delivery timing, staffing, implementation and acceptance.
5. **Acquisition integration:** monitor Blue Fortress contribution, profitability and integration costs.
6. **Dilution:** diluted EPS (1.64 sen) is below basic EPS (1.76 sen) because the report includes 44.15m potential shares from employee share options in the diluted calculation.
7. **Litigation:** Infomina Philippines’ civil case against Bank of the Philippine Islands remains in court exhibit pre-marking, scheduled to continue in October 2026, January 2027 and February 2027.
8. **Market valuation:** this filing alone does not provide a share price, market capitalisation or valuation multiples; valuation and target price should not be concluded from this report alone.

## 7. Preliminary investment view

**Operating momentum: positive.** Revenue, PBT and EPS increased strongly year-on-year, and the disclosed RM614.2m order book plus regional Broadcom appointment provide growth avenues.

**Earnings quality / cash flow: caution.** Negative operating cash flow and the rise in receivables/contract assets are the most important near-term watch items. Margin compression also means profit growth lagged revenue growth.

**Provisional stance: Watch / further research, not a price-based buy call.** Before assigning a valuation or target price, obtain the current share price, shares outstanding/market capitalisation, several years of earnings and cash-flow history, order-book conversion data, and subsequent quarterly collections. No price target is assigned in this record.
