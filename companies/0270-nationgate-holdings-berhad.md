### Management explanation and outlook
- The year-on-year revenue decline was mainly due to lower contribution from the **Data Computing** segment. Management also cited less favourable foreign-exchange movements as a factor behind the sharp Q2 PBT decline.
- Compared with Q1 FY2026, Q2 revenue rose 2.8% to RM1.769b, gross profit rose 106.5% to RM58.4m, and PBT improved from a loss of RM8.4m to a profit of RM8.1m. Management attributed the sequential improvement to stronger Networking & Telecommunications contribution and improved Data Computing performance.
- Management remains cautiously optimistic for FY2026, citing AI, cloud computing, digital infrastructure, server manufacturing, high-speed data transmission and photonics opportunities. Photonics-related facilities were expected to be completed in Q4 2026.
- The report states the interim report is unaudited.

### Segment and geographic concentration
- Data Computing revenue was RM1.503b in Q2 and RM3.024b in 1H FY2026, versus RM2.419b and RM5.079b respectively a year earlier. It remains the dominant segment and the principal source of the reported revenue decline.
- Malaysia contributed RM1.377b of Q2 revenue; other Asian countries contributed RM211.8m and the USA RM117.1m. The geographic mix shifted materially versus the prior-year quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt and cash flow
- Cash and bank balances: RM321.1m, down from RM417.5m at 31 December 2025.
- Total borrowings: RM901.9m; total liabilities RM2.229b; total equity RM1.028b.
- Reported gearing ratio increased to 0.88x from 0.70x at year-end 2025.
- 1H operating cash flow was **negative RM97.9m**; investing cash flow was negative RM88.4m, including RM93.8m of PPE purchases; financing cash flow was positive RM89.8m.
- Contracted capital commitments for PPE were RM362.8m, versus RM98.5m a year earlier.
- Trade receivables rose to RM1.522b from RM814.2m at year-end 2025, while inventories fell to RM684.1m from RM1.141b. Monitor receivables collection and working-capital conversion.

### Dividends

## Investment interpretation (analysis, not company guidance)
**Overall: weak earnings momentum, with sequential stabilisation but significant execution and balance-sheet watchpoints.**
1. **Positive:** Q2 improved from Q1, and the company is investing in photonics capabilities tied to AI/data transmission demand.
2. **Negative:** Revenue and profit remain sharply below the prior year; 1H PBT was slightly negative and operating cash flow was negative.
3. **Financial risk:** Borrowings and gearing rose, cash declined, receivables increased substantially, and capital commitments expanded. These factors make cash conversion and project returns important to monitor.
4. **Concentration risk:** Data Computing is the main revenue driver, so its recovery or continued weakness is likely to be important for future results.
5. **FX risk:** The report describes USD-denominated purchases and USD forward contracts with notional value RM2.238b (equivalent to USD548m) at 30 June 2026. Hedge effectiveness and FX impacts should be followed.
