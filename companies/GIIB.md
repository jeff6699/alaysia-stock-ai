# GIIB Holdings Berhad (GIIB) — Q2 2026 Financial Review

- **Stock:** GIIB Holdings Berhad
- **Reporting period:** Quarter and six months ended 30 June 2026
- **Announcement date:** 14 August 2026
- **Source:** GIIB Holdings Berhad, *Interim Financial Statements for the Period Ended 30 June 2026 (2nd Quarter)*, 15 pages
- **Status:** Unaudited interim results
- **Research note date:** 19 October 2026

## Executive summary

GIIB's Q2 FY2026 loss narrowed substantially year-on-year, but revenue fell sharply and operating cash flow remained negative. Liquidity constraints, high short-term borrowings and material litigation remain important risks. The results do not yet establish a sustainable operating turnaround.

Calculated indicators:
- Q2 revenue declined about 59.0% year-on-year; six-month revenue declined about 59.2%.
- Q2 gross margin was about 12.3%, versus about 17.5% in Q2 FY2025.
- Six-month net loss narrowed about 55.6% year-on-year.
- Q2 other income was RM2.008m, compared with RM0.038m a year earlier. Its composition should be checked before treating the lower loss as recurring improvement.

Management said the current quarter's loss was the lowest quarterly loss in nearly two years. It attributed the lower loss mainly to operating-efficiency improvements, tighter cost controls and reversal of certain provisions/accruals no longer required, while noting that liquidity affected revenue.

The company remains cash-flow constrained despite the narrower accounting loss.

## Business and outlook

GIIB's core rubber compounding business supplies technical compounds under Rubtek™ and retreading solutions under Supercool™. Management described demand from commercial fleet operators as stable, and highlighted sustainability/circular-economy opportunities. Risks cited include global trade uncertainty, supply-chain changes, and raw-material and freight-cost fluctuations.

Items to monitor:
1. Collaboration with Global Enviro Pte Ltd and Stamford Tyres Pte Ltd on green tyres and retread materials in Singapore remained in material/tyre testing stage.
2. A proposed investment in Tricrest Foodie Trading Sdn. Bhd. relating to Malaysian distribution and branding rights for Wonlucky products was at MOU stage; a conditional share subscription agreement was targeted within 90 days of the 26 June 2026 MOU.
3. A July 2026 MOU with Zhejiang Farizon Intelligent Technology and CAF Motors outlined a framework for a commercial-vehicle 4S Hub in Peninsular Malaysia; definitive agreements remained subject to terms and conditions.
4. These are MOUs/proposed arrangements, not evidence in this report of completed revenue-generating contracts.

## Material risks and governance

- **Litigation:** The report describes a disputed RM14.394m claim involving GIIB Healthcare Products Sdn. Bhd. and Glomaxes Latex Glove Manufacturer Sdn. Bhd. It states that on 31 July 2026 the Shah Alam High Court allowed the winding-up petition and awarded RM20,000 costs to be borne equally by the respondent and intervener. The practical financial and subsidiary-level implications require close monitoring.
- **Other litigation:** GIIB's separate suit seeking, among other relief, RM103.583m damages was described as ongoing; the report says the trial was ongoing.
- **Audit qualification:** The auditors expressed a qualified opinion on the audited financial statements for the financial period ended 31 December 2025; the announcement was made on 29 April 2026.
- **No dividend** was declared or proposed for the period.
- The report states there were no contingent assets or liabilities arising since the last audited financial statements, but separately describes the above litigation. Investors should review the full legal disclosures and subsequent announcements rather than assume litigation has no financial consequences.

## Investment interpretation

**Current stance: High-risk watchlist; turnaround not yet confirmed.**

Positive signs:
- Quarterly and half-year losses narrowed year-on-year.
- Management reports lower cost base and improved operational efficiency.
- Potential optionality from green-tyre collaborations and proposed commercial-vehicle/distribution initiatives.

Negative signs:
- Revenue down roughly 59% year-on-year.
- Gross margin contracted.
- Negative operating cash flow and low cash balances.
- RM13.961m short-term borrowings and total borrowings of RM17.995m.
- Material litigation and prior-year audit qualification.
- Placement shares increase the share count and may dilute existing holders.

Do not make a valuation or buy/sell conclusion from book value alone. The report's net assets per share were RM0.056; any price-to-book comparison requires a verified market price and consideration of asset quality, liquidity, legal exposures and the path to positive operating cash flow.
