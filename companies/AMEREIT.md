# AME Real Estate Investment Trust (AME REIT) — Company Research Record

> **Latest source:** [Q2 FY2027 Interim Report Summary](../sources/AMEREIT-Q2-FY2027.md), quarter ended 30 September 2026 (unaudited).  
> **Archive date:** 21 October 2026.  
> **Basis:** Figures are based on the company’s unaudited interim report. RM amounts are in RM million unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Executive summary

AME REIT's Q2 FY2027 report shows solid rental and net property income growth, driven by acquisitions and higher rents on tenancy renewals. Six-month rental income increased 14.6% YoY to RM34.182m and NPI increased 15.3% to RM31.242m. Distributable income increased 6.0% to RM23.158m, with six-month DPU of 4.34 sen versus 4.12 sen a year earlier.

Net income growth was boosted by RM5.047m in unrealised investment-property fair-value gains. Meanwhile, six-month financing costs rose 40.9% YoY to RM6.688m and operating cash flow declined to RM24.727m from RM36.878m. These factors make recurring income, cash conversion and financing costs more decision-useful than headline net profit alone.

**Investment read-through:** occupancy and rental growth are strengths, but the next reports should show that acquisition-led expansion translates into sustainable distributable income per unit without excessive financing pressure.

## 3. Balance sheet and financing

At 30 September 2026, investment properties were RM1,051.100m, total assets RM1,104.213m, financing RM375.478m, cash RM36.368m and NAV RM675.225m. NAV per unit was RM1.2668 before the proposed distribution and RM1.2449 after it. Total financing increased 28.4% from 31 March 2026. Financing/total assets was approximately 34.0% as a simple ratio; this is not presented as the formal regulatory gearing ratio.

## 5. Portfolio and strategy

The portfolio comprised 44 investment properties: 38 industrial properties, 3 industrial-related properties and 3 industrial land parcels held for development. The manager reported 100% occupancy for existing industrial and industrial-related properties, excluding development land. Three industrial land parcels totalling 8.87 acres were acquired for RM50.23m, with completion on 23 September 2026. The disposal of i-Park SILC 3, 4 and 6 completed on 7 May 2026 for RM14.47m, producing a reported net realised gain of RM3.48m.

## 6. Risks and next-quarter watchlist

1. **Financing costs:** monitor debt and Islamic financing costs after the increase to RM6.688m for the half-year.
2. **Operating cash flow:** cash from operations declined despite higher accounting profit.
3. **Acquisition returns:** assess rental contribution and NPI generated by acquired properties and development land.
4. **Recurring DPU:** compare realised income and distributable income per unit over equivalent periods.
5. **Unrealised gains:** separate fair-value gains from recurring cash income.
6. **Occupancy and rent renewals:** monitor whether the reported 100% occupancy and higher renewal rates persist.
7. **Related-party transactions:** review rental income and property acquisitions involving entities related to the manager.

## 7. Analyst conclusion

**Positive operating trend, with financing and cash conversion as key watch items.** Rental income and NPI growth are encouraging, but headline net income was supported by unrealised gains and operating cash flow weakened. The investment case depends on maintaining occupancy, translating acquisitions into recurring income per unit and managing financing costs.
