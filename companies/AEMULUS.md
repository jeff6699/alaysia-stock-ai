## Company Overview

Aemulus is a Malaysian semiconductor test-equipment company focused on the design and development of automated test equipment (ATE), test and measurement instruments, and related consultancy/test services. Its exposure includes RF and mixed-signal semiconductor testing, automotive electronics, CMOS image sensors (CIS), and increasingly Data Centre / AI applications. The group was founded by engineers with multinational electronics-industry backgrounds and operates through Aemulus Corporation Sdn Bhd, its wholly owned operating subsidiary. citeturn1search5turn1search8

## Investment Thesis — Current View

AEMULUS is being tracked as a **high-growth / high-risk semiconductor equipment candidate**.

The most important current theme is the company's increasing exposure to **AI/Data Centre semiconductor testing**, supported by repeated new-order announcements in 2026. The investment case depends on whether these orders translate into sustained revenue growth and, more importantly, durable gross-profit and cash-flow improvement.

### Key growth drivers

- Data Centre / AI semiconductor test-system demand.
- CIS testing demand in China and other Asian markets.
- Automotive semiconductor testing.
- Expansion of ATE product platforms.
- Commercialisation of strategic customer relationships.
- Production-capacity expansion following the Revotronix acquisition/integration.

## Key Risks

1. **Order value ≠ profit.** Margin on each test system must be monitored.
2. **Delivery timing risk.** Orders are recognised progressively and may shift between quarters.
3. **Working-capital risk.** Semiconductor equipment growth can require significant inventory and receivables funding.
4. **Customer concentration risk.** Large orders from a limited number of customers can create earnings volatility.
5. **Semiconductor cycle risk.** AI strength does not eliminate cyclical weakness in other semiconductor segments.
6. **Execution risk.** Materials, supply chain and production capacity can affect delivery.
7. **Valuation risk.** If the market prices AEMULUS as a major AI beneficiary before earnings fully catch up, downside can remain high.
