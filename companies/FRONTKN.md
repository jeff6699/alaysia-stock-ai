## Business and operating context
Frontken provides specialised engineering, maintenance and support services, with semiconductor-related operations and oil-and-gas-related services. In Q3 FY2026, management cited stronger semiconductor volume and orders in Taiwan, higher semiconductor volume in Singapore, and oil-and-gas supply-related contributions in Malaysia.

Quarter-on-quarter, Q3 revenue fell 3% from RM186.906m in Q2, while PBT rose from RM73.813m to RM76.940m and PAT rose from RM52.460m to RM61.382m. Management attributed the revenue decline mainly to lower Malaysian oil-and-gas supply-related sales, and the PAT improvement mainly to higher-margin semiconductor business.

## Segment and geographic observations
- Taiwan external revenue was RM132.242m in Q3, about 73% of group external revenue; 9M Taiwan external revenue was RM366.124m.
- Management said Taiwan revenue grew 11% YoY in RM terms for Q3 and would have grown 24% on a like-for-like New Taiwan Dollar basis. 9M Taiwan revenue grew 10% in RM terms, or 21% in New Taiwan Dollar terms.
- Malaysia revenue grew 18% YoY in Q3, mainly from oil-and-gas supply-related activities.
- Singapore benefited from an overall pick-up in semiconductor volumes.
- Semiconductor segment outlook is supported by AI, agentic/generative AI, HPC, automotive electronics and advanced manufacturing demand, according to management.

## Investment interpretation — provisional
**Positive:** strong reported revenue and profit growth; management's semiconductor outlook is constructive; substantial cash, short-term investments and low bank borrowing provide balance-sheet flexibility.

**Monitor:** valuation may already price in semiconductor/AI growth; Taiwan represents a large share of revenue; foreign-exchange translation affected reported growth; receivables and other working capital absorbed cash; warrant exercises increased the share count; planned land/property spending could be material.

Do not infer that the stock is undervalued from operating performance alone. A fair-value and margin-of-safety conclusion requires a verified current share price, share count basis, and explicit earnings/valuation assumptions.
