Key observation:
- Revenue grew modestly, but gross profit declined, indicating pressure on gross margin.

- PAT nevertheless increased to RM1.120m from RM1.058m.
- 2Q gross margin was approximately 19.61%, versus approximately 20.26% previously.

## Investment Interpretation

### Positive

1. Revenue continues to grow modestly, with 1HFY2027 revenue +3.22% YoY.
2. 2QFY2027 PAT improved 5.86% YoY.
3. Selling and distribution expenses were reduced materially.
4. Interest expense was lower year-on-year.
5. Equity increased to RM204.884m.
6. Current assets remain above current liabilities on the reported balance sheet.

### Concerns

1. Gross profit fell 20.94% YTD despite revenue growth.
2. 1HFY2027 gross margin compressed to approximately 17.35% from 22.65%.
3. 1HFY2027 PAT fell 17.28%.
4. Trade receivables increased sharply from RM31.328m to RM56.938m.
5. Inventories increased from RM10.490m to RM22.087m.
6. Operating cash flow was negative RM17.859m.
7. Borrowings increased substantially, with current borrowings rising from RM1.262m to RM23.303m.
8. The business therefore needs stronger cash conversion and gross-margin recovery before a bullish earnings thesis can be established.

## FY2026 Distortion / Normalization Context

The cash-flow statement shows that FY2026 included very large non-cash impairment charges, including:
- Goodwill impairment: RM206.327m
- Property, plant and equipment impairment: RM17.731m

These FY2026 audited impairment items materially distorted the prior-year reported earnings comparison. Future valuation work should therefore distinguish between:
- reported historical earnings,
- normalized earnings after exceptional/non-cash impairment effects, and
- actual cash generation.

The 2QFY2027 filing itself does not provide a full normalized-earnings bridge, so no normalized EPS or fair value should be inferred from this filing alone.
