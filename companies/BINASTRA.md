## 1. Executive summary

BINASTRA reported strong year-on-year growth in Q2 FY2027, supported by its newly acquired subsidiary LF Lansen Sdn Bhd, solar installation projects, and higher revenue from conventional construction and data-centre projects. The group reported an outstanding order book of approximately RM6.7 billion, expected to provide earnings visibility over the next four financial years.

Key watchpoints are working-capital intensity, the sharp increase in trade and other receivables, project execution and cost inflation. Strong accounting profit should be assessed alongside cash conversion and collection of receivables.

## 3. What drove the results

According to the company's report:
- Construction segment Q2 revenue was RM658.2 million and PBT was RM70.6 million.
- LF Lansen Sdn Bhd, a newly acquired subsidiary, contributed to revenue growth.
- Solar installation projects contributed RM97.4 million of revenue during the quarter.
- Revenue from conventional construction and data-centre projects increased by RM164.2 million to RM560.8 million.
- Sequentially, Q2 revenue was RM52.8 million above the quarter ended 30 April 2026; owners' net profit increased from RM35.3 million to RM50.6 million.

## 4. Order book and outlook

- Outstanding order book: approximately RM6.7 billion.
- During the quarter, the group secured a material letter of award worth RM0.5 billion for a proposed data-centre development.
- Management expects the order book to provide earnings visibility over the next four financial years.
- The company cites Malaysian infrastructure investment, industrial expansion, logistics facilities and data-centre demand—particularly in Johor and Selangor—as potential supports.
- Management identified material-price fluctuations, labour shortages, regulatory changes and geopolitical uncertainty as operating challenges.
- The group intends to integrate LF Lansen, strengthen specialised engineering capabilities, improve working-capital management and expand its data-centre presence.

## 7. Investment interpretation (analysis, not company guidance)

**Positives**
1. Strong revenue and earnings growth.
2. Improved quarterly gross margin.
3. RM6.7 billion order book provides project visibility.
4. Exposure to data-centre construction and solar installation.
5. Operating cash flow increased year-on-year.

**Risks / checks**
1. Receivables are large and increased materially; track ageing and collection.
2. Construction margins can be sensitive to materials, labour and project execution.
3. Growth includes acquisition contribution, so separate organic growth from acquired growth where disclosures allow.
4. Order book is not the same as guaranteed profit or cash flow; assess contract terms and conversion.
5. Corporate guarantees are material relative to equity and should be monitored.
6. The report alone does not establish whether the shares are cheap or expensive.
