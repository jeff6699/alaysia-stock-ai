## Executive summary

Q2 2026 revenue was RM4,943m, down 4% year-on-year, while profit attributable to the Company's equity holders rose to RM987m from RM505m (+95%). The uplift was mainly driven by Industrial Development, including a RM529m share of gain from the disposal of 935 acres in Kulai estate, and improved Downstream results. This quarter's earnings should therefore not be treated as entirely recurring.

For 1H2026, revenue was RM9,633m (-4% YoY), profit before tax RM2,067m (+31%), profit for the period RM1,616m (+39%), and profit attributable to equity holders RM1,547m (+44%). Recurring PBIT rose to RM2,120m from RM1,622m, with Industrial Development contributing RM689m, including the Kulai land-sale gain.

## Land transactions and subsequent events

- During Q2, the Group completed the disposal of 935 acres in Kulai estate, Johor, to Eco Business Park 8, recognising RM529m PBIT as the Group's share of the gain.
- On 15 July 2026, the Group entered conditional SPAs to dispose of 1,021.93 acres in Bukit Kerayong estate, Kuala Selangor, for RM798.31m. The report states the land was reclassified as assets held for sale.
- On 3 August 2026, disposal of 68.68 hectares in Lambak estate, Johor, for RM82.8m was completed; the financial effects and gain were expected to be recognised in a subsequent quarter.

## Management outlook stated in the report

Management expects tightening global supply and resilient demand to support CPO prices. Potential supporting factors cited include possible El Niño conditions toward year-end, Indonesia's B50 biodiesel mandate, and firm crude oil prices amid geopolitical tensions. The company remains positive on FY2026, citing market dynamics, operational resilience and Industrial Development execution, while acknowledging market and weather uncertainties.

## Key risks and items to monitor

1. **Earnings quality:** Q2/1H profit benefited materially from land-disposal gains; assess underlying earnings excluding these gains.
2. **Plantation fundamentals:** 1H Upstream PBIT declined 18%; monitor CPO/PK realised prices, FFB production, extraction rates, costs and weather.
3. **Indonesia land compliance exposure:** The report describes allegations of overlapping plantation areas involving certain subsidiaries and ongoing verification with Indonesia's Forestry Enforcement Task Force. As at the report, no administrative sanction decree had been issued; final outcome remained unresolved.
4. **Leverage and liquidity:** Borrowings rose to RM7,555m and cash declined to RM531m after the Sukuk redemption and other funding movements.
5. **Execution of planned land disposals:** Track conditions, completion, cash proceeds and when gains are recognised.
6. **Downstream margins:** Improvement in Asia Pacific bulk operations is partly offset by softer demand and margins in differentiated refinery operations.

## Investor interpretation (analysis, not company guidance)

- **Positive:** Strong reported profit growth; downstream recovery; industrial land development provides diversification; management is constructive on the CPO outlook.
- **Caution:** Reported earnings growth is partly transaction-driven and may not recur at the same scale. Core upstream earnings weakened in 1H, operating cash flow declined YoY, and borrowings increased.
- **What to verify next quarter:** Adjusted/recurring earnings excluding land gains; CPO and PK prices; FFB output and OER; downstream margins; operating cash flow; debt and cash trend; progress on land sales; and updates on Indonesia land verification.
