## Capital Commitments

Contracted but not provided-for PPE commitments as at 30 June 2026:

- RM1.388bn

This increased materially from RM357.049m at 31 December 2025.

Investment implication:
- Positive: substantial committed fleet/capacity expansion can support future revenue and earnings.
- Risk: execution requires significant capital and may increase depreciation, financing needs and operating leverage.

## Outlook From Management

Management expects:

1. Container shipping to remain stable and resilient despite geopolitical uncertainty.
2. Intra-Asia routes to experience relatively limited disruption.
3. Two new chemical tankers scheduled for delivery by year-end to expand fleet capacity and service offerings.
4. The new tankers are expected to contribute positively to future earnings.
5. Logistics to continue improving through better utilisation, operational efficiency and demand for integrated logistics solutions.

## Tax Position

The report states that the Group can continue to enjoy a 100% Malaysian shipping-profit tax exemption up to YA2026, subject to meeting the substance requirements under the relevant Gazette Order.

The reported effective tax rate was:
- Q2: 4.75%
- 1H: 4.42%

The low effective tax rate is therefore an important earnings-support factor, but future tax treatment beyond YA2026 should be monitored.

## Investment Interpretation

### Positive factors

1. Strong sequential acceleration: Q2 revenue +20.79%, PBT +45.78%, PAT +44.53% versus Q1.
2. Shipping segment has very strong contribution and remains the core earnings engine.
3. Higher freight rates and slot sales are currently supporting earnings.
4. Large cash balance following IPO provides funding for fleet expansion.
5. RM561.135m of IPO proceeds remained available for vessel acquisitions at quarter end.
6. Two chemical tankers are expected to add capacity and future earnings.
7. 3.20 sen interim dividend provides an early shareholder-return signal.
8. Low effective tax rate remains supportive through YA2026, subject to exemption conditions.

### Key risks

1. Earnings are highly concentrated in shipping; Q2 shipping contributed 97.31% of revenue.
2. Freight-rate strength may be cyclical and should not automatically be extrapolated.
3. Capital commitments of RM1.388bn create significant execution and capital-allocation risk.
4. Operating cash flow of RM166.2m was below PPE/leasehold-land spending of RM212.5m in 1H.
5. Logistics remains loss-making at segment level.
6. Fleet expansion will increase depreciation and potentially financing requirements.
7. The 100% shipping tax exemption is currently stated through YA2026; future policy should be monitored.
8. The report provides no YoY comparable figures, so the earnings trend should initially be evaluated mainly through sequential quarters and future reported periods.
