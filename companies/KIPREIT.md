# KIP Real Estate Investment Trust (KIPREIT) — Company Research Record

> **Source:** Q1FY2027 Interim Financial Report, quarter ended 30 September 2026 (unaudited).  
> **Archive date:** 19 October 2026.  
> **Basis:** Figures below are taken from the attached report; RM amounts are stated in RM million unless noted otherwise.

## 1. Executive summary

KIP REIT delivered strong top-line and net property income growth in Q1FY2027, largely reflecting better performance across its existing malls and contributions from newly acquired assets. However, higher property operating expenses, management fees and borrowing costs absorbed the improvement, leaving profit after tax and distributable income almost unchanged year-on-year. The acquisition of Setapak Central Mall was completed during the quarter and was accompanied by a 200 million-unit placement and higher gearing.

**Investment read-through:** portfolio income is expanding, but investors should monitor whether the acquired assets generate enough incremental net property income to offset additional financing costs and unit dilution. The quarter's distribution per unit was 0.82 sen, versus 1.80 sen in the corresponding quarter last year; the report identifies the current distribution as an advance income distribution covering 1 July–24 August 2026, so the periods are not directly equivalent.

### Main drivers

- Lease income rose 18.7% to RM41.745m; revenue from contracts with customers increased 25.5% to RM7.005m.
- Retail contributed RM45.478m of revenue and RM31.697m of NPI. Industrial contributed RM3.272m of revenue and RM3.255m of NPI.
- The manager attributed higher revenue to better performance from the nine existing KIPMalls and contributions from acquired retail and industrial properties.
- Borrowing costs increased to RM8.663m from RM6.455m, up RM2.208m, following additional borrowings to finance acquisitions.
- Management fees increased to RM6.087m from RM4.556m. Total trust and other expenses also increased.

## 4. Cash flow

- Net cash generated from operating activities was RM23.841m, compared with RM30.936m a year earlier.
- Net cash used in investing activities was RM400.021m, primarily reflecting the RM435m acquisition of investment properties, partly offset by the RM43.5m deposit/related acquisition-cost movement shown in the statement.
- Net cash generated from financing activities was RM357.017m, including RM163m from new units and RM234m of borrowings.
- Cash and cash equivalents at period-end were RM82.294m.

**Interpretation:** the acquisition was substantially funded through a combination of new units and additional debt. Operating cash generation remains positive, but acquisition-led growth has increased leverage and financing-cost exposure.

## 5. Acquisition and portfolio

- **Setapak Central Mall:** proposed acquisition consideration RM435m; completion was announced on 30 September 2026.
- A 60:40 funding structure (bank borrowings and private placement) had been planned. The first placement tranche comprised 200m units issued at RM0.815 per unit on 27 August 2026.
- Portfolio at quarter-end: 20 assets, comprising 14 retail malls and 6 industrial properties.
- Investment properties were not revalued during the quarter, according to the report.
- Capital commitments listed include KIPMall Tampoi RM2.977m, KIPMall Bangi RM0.794m and smaller amounts for several other malls. No Setapak Central Mall commitment remained in the table at 30 September 2026.

## 6. Distribution

- The manager intends to distribute at least 90% of distributable income for FY2027.
- The reported 0.82 sen advance income distribution was for 1 July 2026 to 24 August 2026, with entitlement date 24 August and payment on 24 September 2026.
- Current-period distributable income was RM17.614m. Undistributed distributable income brought forward was RM23.488m; after the proposed/declared RM7.861m distribution, undistributed distributable income was RM33.241m.
- Do not annualise the 0.82 sen DPU or infer a yield without checking the applicable distribution period and a verified market price.

## 7. Risks and monitoring checklist

1. **Leverage and interest rates:** gearing increased to 42.64%; 58% of debt was floating-rate and the weighted-average borrowing rate rose to 4.50%.
2. **Acquisition execution:** track Setapak Central Mall's occupancy, rental contribution and NPI after completion, and whether returns exceed incremental financing costs.
3. **Expense growth:** property operating expenses rose 24.9%, faster than revenue growth of 19.6%.
4. **Per-unit dilution:** units increased by 200m; track realised EPU and DPU on a per-unit basis as acquisition contributions mature.
5. **Distribution comparability:** the 0.82 sen advance distribution covers a shorter period than a full quarter's ordinary distribution; compare equivalent entitlement periods before drawing conclusions.
6. **NAV per unit:** NAV per unit declined versus 30 June 2026; distinguish acquisition/placement effects and distribution effects when reviewing future periods.
7. **Regional performance:** Central Region generated RM23.094m revenue; monitor whether growth is sustained and whether cost growth follows revenue.

## 8. Outlook stated by management

The manager expressed a favourable outlook, expecting stable performance through FY2027 based on the existing portfolio's performance, leasing and operational initiatives, and continued evaluation of retail and industrial investment opportunities. This is management guidance, not a guarantee of future results.

## 9. Analyst conclusion

**Operationally positive, but per-unit and financing metrics require close monitoring.** Revenue and NPI grew strongly, while profit and distributable income remained flat because of higher costs. The acquisition expands the asset base, but leverage and unit count both increased materially. The next reports should demonstrate sustained incremental NPI from acquired assets, stabilising expense ratios, and a distribution profile that is sustainable after accounting for debt service and dilution.
