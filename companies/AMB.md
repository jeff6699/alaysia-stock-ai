## Investment snapshot

AMB delivered moderate top-line and profit growth in 1H2026. Insurance revenue increased 7.0% YoY to RM3.261b, profit before tax rose 4.8% to RM595.5m, and profit after tax increased 4.4% to RM446.0m. Life insurance was the main profit-growth driver; general insurance revenue grew but its pre-tax profit declined.

**Preliminary view:** Fundamentally resilient, with positive revenue growth and strong investment assets, but earnings growth is modest and the general insurance claims ratio is worsening. Valuation and a firm buy/hold/sell conclusion require the current share price, market capitalisation, full dividend history and peer comparison.

## Segment analysis

### General insurance
- 1H insurance revenue: RM1.844b, +5.5% YoY.
- 1H gross written premium: RM2.002b, +6.7%.
- 1H pre-tax profit: RM298.2m, -5.0%.
- Claims ratio increased to 60.4% from 55.4% (+5.0 percentage points).
- Expense ratio rose to 25.3% from 24.2%.
- Combined ratio was 87.6% versus 86.9%.
- Management said premium growth outperformed industry growth (6.5% vs 1.4%), supported by agency and franchise channels.

**Interpretation:** Premium growth is healthy, but rising claims and expenses, plus lower segment profit, need monitoring.

### Life insurance
- 1H insurance revenue: RM1.417b, +9.0% YoY.
- 1H gross written premium: RM2.364b, +16.0%.
- 1H annualised new premium (ANP): RM487.6m, +8.7%.
- 1H pre-tax profit: RM294.1m, +14.4%.
- Expense ratio improved to 10.8% from 11.4%.
- Block persistency ratio declined to 81.5% from 84.8%.

**Interpretation:** Life insurance is the strongest profit-growth contributor, helped by investment-linked protection and employee-benefit business. Persistency is a watch item.

## Dividends
The report states that an interim dividend for FY2026 of **35.5 sen per ordinary share** was paid on 10 June 2026. It also records FY2025 interim dividends of 89.5 sen per ordinary share paid on 13 February 2026. These figures are the dividends specified in this report, not a forecast of future distributions.

## Legal / contingent-liability update
The report notes that on 18 August 2026 the Court of Appeal dismissed MyCC's application for leave to appeal concerning the Competition Appeal Tribunal decision in the general-insurance trade-discount/labour-rate case, with costs. AGIC's portion of costs was RM5,000. Based on this report, the latest disclosed decision is favourable to AGIC; continue to check later disclosures for any subsequent developments.

## Management outlook
Management highlighted Malaysia's economic growth of 6.0% in 2Q2026 and expects to focus on distribution capabilities, productivity, underwriting and claims discipline, digital initiatives and customer experience. The company did not issue a profit forecast or profit guarantee.

## Risks and monitoring checklist
1. **Claims inflation / underwriting:** Track general-insurance claims ratio and combined ratio.
2. **Investment volatility:** 1H net investment income benefited from stronger FVTPL results; results may fluctuate with markets.
3. **Life-policy persistency:** Ratio fell to 81.5%; watch renewals and customer retention.
4. **Growth versus industry:** Life ANP grew 8.7%, below the stated industry growth of 14.0%.
5. **Valuation:** Add current share price, market cap, P/E, P/B and dividend yield before judging margin of safety.
6. **Capital and regulation:** Continue monitoring insurance capital adequacy and regulatory updates; this interim report alone does not establish the current solvency ratio.
