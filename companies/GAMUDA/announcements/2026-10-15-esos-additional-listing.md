## Investment interpretation

1. **Dilution appears small in share-count terms:** about 0.18%, all else equal. Actual EPS impact depends on earnings and the period the new shares are outstanding.
2. **Cash proceeds:** option exercise brings the company approximately RM37.23 million in gross proceeds based on the disclosed figures; this is not operating profit.
3. **Exercise prices vary:** the option exercise prices range from RM1.275 to RM4.220. Do not interpret these prices alone as a valuation signal or as evidence of insider buying in the open market.
4. **Price context:** the KLSE Screener page showed GAMUDA at RM4.90 when retrieved; this is a page snapshot, not a verified closing price for the announcement date.
5. **What to monitor:** issued share capital, future ESOS exercises, diluted EPS, project execution, order book conversion and cash flow.

## Preliminary assessment

**Corporate-action impact: neutral to mildly dilutive.** The share-count effect is limited, while the proceeds are a modest capital inflow. This announcement alone is not a buy or sell signal; investment decisions should be based on valuation, earnings outlook, order book and cash generation.
