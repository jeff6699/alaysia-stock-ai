# Westports Holdings Berhad (WPRTS 5246)

## Business overview
Westports develops and operates port infrastructure, with activities predominantly in Malaysia. The group identifies port development and management of port operations as its sole operating segment.

## Latest quarterly update

### Q3 2026 / 9M2026
- Reporting period ended 30 September 2026; unaudited.
- 9M2026 reported revenue RM2.832b (+37% YoY); operational revenue excluding construction revenue RM2.359b (+26%).
- 9M2026 PBT RM1.348b (+43%); PAT attributable to owners RM1.030b (+42%); EPS 30.00 sen (+42%).
- Q3 operational revenue RM797.7m (+20% YoY, -1% QoQ); PAT RM342.6m (+26% YoY, -5% QoQ).
- Management cited tariff increases and stronger VAS for YoY growth. QoQ moderation was attributed mainly to lower container storage revenue after long-staying metal and e-waste container volumes normalised.
- 9M operating cash flow RM1.445b; investing cash outflow RM472.0m; cash and short-term investments RM952.7m; borrowings RM980.0m.
- Service concession obligation RM2.317b and lease liabilities RM141.4m should be tracked separately from conventional borrowings.
- Expansion of CT10–CT17 is ongoing; dredging and reclamation began in 2025, with CT10 expected to be ready in 2028. Contracted capital commitments were RM865.9m.
- Management expects 2026 container throughput to be at least the prior year's level, while warning that evolving conditions could materially affect volumes.

**Assessment:** Strong year-on-year operating performance and cash generation, offset by sequential Q3 moderation and the capital intensity/execution risks of terminal expansion. This report alone does not establish whether the share is undervalued; valuation requires a verified current price and scenario assumptions.
