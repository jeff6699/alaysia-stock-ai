# RCE Capital Berhad (Bursa: 9296) — FY2027 Q2 Interim Results

- **Report period:** Quarter and six months ended 30 September 2026
- **Announcement date:** 19 November 2026
- **Source:** Company Interim Financial Report, 23 pages, unaudited
- **Source file:** `Qtr Ann 30 09 2026.pdf`

## 1. Executive summary

RCE Capital's FY2027 first-half revenue was essentially flat year-on-year, while pre-tax profit rose 8.4% and net profit rose 13.3%. Profitability improved mainly alongside lower profit/interest expense and lower impairment allowances. Q2 revenue and profit both improved sequentially. Credit quality remains a key risk to monitor given the financing-receivables business and the large Stage 3 expected-credit-loss allowance.

## 3. Sequential performance

Compared with Q1 FY2027, Q2 revenue increased 7.2% (RM84.021m vs RM78.386m), PBT rose 6.7% (RM43.034m vs RM40.335m), and net profit rose 5.9% (RM32.206m vs RM30.426m). Management attributed this to higher disbursements, higher fee income and customer refinancing/early-settlement income, plus lower impairment allowances due mainly to lower non-performing financing. Sales and marketing expenses increased as the group sought to boost disbursement.

## 4. Management explanation and outlook

- Q2 revenue improvement was primarily driven by higher early-settlement income from greater customer refinancing, partly offset by lower payroll collection processing and administration fee income.
- For the six months, higher pre- and post-tax profits reflected lower profit/interest expense and lower impairment allowances, partly offset by lower income from deposit placements.
- Management prioritises disciplined/selective credit assessments, customer acquisition, strategic partnerships and digital initiatives to improve operating efficiency.
- The group expects to remain profitable for FY ending 31 March 2027. No profit forecast or profit guarantee was provided.

## 5. Balance sheet and funding

As at 30 September 2026:
- Total assets: RM2.912bn (31 March 2026: RM3.004bn)
- Total equity: RM879.990m (31 March 2026: RM870.654m)
- Total liabilities: RM2.032bn (31 March 2026: RM2.133bn)
- Net assets per share: RM0.60 (31 March 2026: RM0.59)
- Financing receivables, net: RM1.959bn
- Financing liabilities: RM1.927bn, down from RM2.111bn at 30 September 2025, mainly due to RM150m net Sukuk redemptions.
- Weighted-average profit/interest rates on financing liabilities ranged from 3.8% to 5.3% p.a.; RM966.095m was fixed-rate and RM960.788m floating-rate.

The business is leveraged by nature as a financing provider. Monitor funding costs, refinancing access and the spread between financing income and funding expense.

## 6. Credit risk

At 30 September 2026, gross financing receivables and loans were RM2.095bn, with impairment allowances of RM135.163m, leaving net receivables of RM1.959bn. Stage 3 allowance was RM89.970m versus RM89.875m at 1 April 2026; the report records RM24.893m transferred into Stage 3 and RM14.977m written off during the period. The total allowance declined from RM136.479m at 1 April to RM135.163m at 30 September.

Although the period's net impairment expense was lower year-on-year, the Stage 3 balance and movement into Stage 3 warrant continued monitoring. The report says the ECL model incorporated updated economic scenarios, including forecast real GDP.

## 7. Cash flow

For 1H FY2027:
- Net cash from operating activities: RM73.896m (prior year RM62.041m)
- Net cash used in financing activities: RM93.127m (prior year RM80.536m)
- Net change in cash and cash equivalents: -RM9.409m (prior year -RM25.621m)
- Cash and cash equivalents at period end: RM12.304m, after excluding deposits assigned to trustees and pledged to financial institutions from total deposits and bank balances of RM683.573m.

The low reported unrestricted cash-equivalent balance should be read in context of the group's financing model and restricted/pledged deposits, not treated as equivalent to a typical industrial company's cash balance.

## 8. Dividend

The board declared a first interim dividend of 3.00 sen per share for FY2027:
- Entitlement date: 8 December 2026
- Payment date: 21 December 2026
- Estimated total payout: RM44.041m

The report also records a 3.50 sen second interim dividend for FY2026 paid on 30 June 2026. The 3.00 sen FY2027 interim dividend is a declared distribution, not a forecast of the full-year dividend.

## 9. Corporate actions and dilution

A new Employees' Share Scheme (ESS), of up to 15% of issued shares excluding treasury shares at any point, became effective on 16 October 2026, replacing the previous ESS. Subsequent to the period, 29.052m options were offered at an exercise price of RM0.775. Potential dilution should be tracked alongside future earnings growth.

## 10. Investment interpretation — preliminary

**Positives**
- Double-digit first-half net-profit and EPS growth.
- Sequential improvement in revenue, PBT and net profit.
- Lower first-half impairment expense and reduced financing liabilities year-on-year.
- Interim dividend declared.

**Risks / watch items**
- Revenue growth is nearly flat; profit growth partly depends on expense and impairment trends.
- Credit deterioration, Stage 3 movements, write-offs and collection performance.
- Funding-cost and refinancing risk, particularly with approximately half of financing liabilities floating-rate.
- ESS-related dilution.
- No valuation conclusion can be made without a verified share price and a valuation comparison.

## 11. Next-quarter monitoring checklist

1. Revenue and financing disbursement growth; distinguish recurring fees from early-settlement/refinancing income.
2. Net impairment expense, Stage 2/Stage 3 balances, write-offs and collection trends.
3. Financing income versus financing expense and deposit-placement income.
4. Financing liabilities, Sukuk maturities, funding rates and floating-rate exposure.
5. EPS progression, dividend coverage and ESS dilution.
6. Share price, trailing/forward P/E, dividend yield and price-to-book against historical and peer ranges before forming a buy-price estimate.
