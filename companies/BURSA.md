## Derivatives Market

1H2026:

- Total contracts: 12.57m, +9.9%
- Average daily contracts: 106,518, +9.9%

Key weakness:
- Segment expenses increased 12.4% to RM41.4m, mainly due to SC regulatory fees and higher staff costs.
- Therefore, higher trading activity did not translate into higher segment profit.

## Management Outlook

Management stated that Bank Negara Malaysia maintained its 2026 Malaysian GDP growth forecast at 4%-5%, while the World Bank revised its Malaysia 2026 growth forecast to 4.4% from 4.1%.

The Exchange remains cautiously optimistic that domestic economic conditions and sustained investment activity should support Malaysia's capital market, while recognising global economic and geopolitical risks.

Strategic priorities include:
1. Enhancing market vibrancy and corporate value creation.
2. MY Value Up Programme and proposed enhancement of Malaysia's benchmark-index ecosystem.
3. Developing the Exchange's Strategic Roadmap.
4. Broadening investor participation and cross-border market connectivity.
5. Advancing the Islamic Market ecosystem, including Bursa Gold Dinar.
6. Continuing sustainability-related products, services and reporting initiatives.

As at 30 June 2026, total IPO fundraising was RM5.4bn and IPO market capitalisation reached RM26.1bn, exceeding the Exchange's initial expectations.

## Investment Interpretation

### Positive factors

1. Earnings are accelerating: 2Q PBT +27.8% YoY and 1H PBT +16.9%.
2. Securities Market remains the dominant earnings engine.
3. Higher ADV and trading velocity are directly supporting trading revenue.
4. Listing and issuer services grew strongly at +64.2% in 1H.
5. IPO activity and market capitalisation are expanding.
6. Islamic Market and Bursa Gold Dinar provide additional growth avenues.
7. No conventional borrowings were reported as at 30 June 2026.
8. Operating cash flow remained strong at RM187.4m in 1H.
9. Interim dividend increased to 16.5 sen from 14.0 sen.
10. Management expects domestic capital-market conditions to remain supportive, while acknowledging global/geopolitical risks.

### Key risks

1. Earnings remain highly sensitive to stock-market activity, trading value and investor participation.
2. Securities Market costs increased sharply because of new SC regulatory fees and higher staff costs.
3. Data Business revenue declined 5.9% in 1H.
4. Derivatives segment profit fell 9.1% despite higher contract volumes.
5. Global market volatility and geopolitical events can reduce trading activity or investor risk appetite.
6. A strong market cycle can make current earnings growth difficult to sustain if ADV normalises.
7. Regulatory changes can directly affect the cost structure.
