# Catalyst Engine V1.5

## Purpose
Identify company-specific or external events that could change earnings, cash flows, moat, risk or valuation. A catalyst is a testable possible driver, not a promise or proof of upside. Label facts, analysis, assumptions, inference and forecasts separately.

Each entry includes evidence source/date/locator, execution dependencies, counter-evidence, and whether the event is company-specific or external. Potential Impact is Low / Medium / High or a supported quantified sensitivity. Probability is a sourced estimate or qualitative Low / Medium / High / N/A with rationale and time horizon; no invented precision. Management targets are FACT only as statements made, not as achieved outcomes.

## Time horizon
- **Near-term:** expected within 12 months.
- **Medium-term:** more than 12 and up to 36 months.
- **Long-term:** beyond 36 months or timing not near/medium but supportable.
These are reporting buckets, not forecasts of occurrence. If timing is unknown, state N/A rather than forcing a bucket.

## Catalyst points (0–10)
Score each of the 10 categories separately, maximum 1 point:
- **1 point:** specific, evidence-backed catalyst; responsible party/path and timeframe are identifiable; confirmation test is observable; material dependency and counter-evidence are disclosed.
- **0.5 point:** plausible and relevant, but one or more key timing, probability, execution, or confirmation elements remains conditional.
- **0 points:** adequate review finds no relevant catalyst, or evidence shows the claim is unsupported/unlikely. State which.
- **N/A:** evidence review is unavailable or too incomplete to judge; do not treat N/A as zero.

Catalyst component = sum of earned points / 10; Available Points equal one point per scoreable category. Do not increase score because several catalysts are duplicates, correlated, or repeat the same expected earnings benefit. A single event may be listed in multiple dimensions for completeness, but explain linkage and avoid duplicate points when it is the same underlying event; mark the overlapping category N/A or score only the best-fitting category and state why.

Catalyst points measure evidence quality and testability, not upside size or event probability alone. Scenario probabilities are separate and must total 100% in decision/bull_base_bear.md.

## Monitoring and audit
Record confirmation signal, source, expected check date/event and what failure means for thesis. After an expected date passes, update probability and evidence; do not leave stale catalysts active. Distinguish a catalyst from a durable moat or base-case assumption.
