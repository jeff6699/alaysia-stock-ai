# Portfolio Monitoring Rules V2.0

## Monitor
1. earnings and guidance
2. revenue / EPS / FCF trend
3. debt and liquidity
4. moat changes
5. valuation gap
6. catalysts
7. critical risks
8. corporate actions

## Alert levels
- GREEN: no material change
- YELLOW: review required
- RED: thesis or risk may have changed

## Alert principle
Only surface changes that can affect the investment decision. Avoid noise.
