# Position Rules V2.0

## Objective
Turn research outputs into disciplined position decisions without replacing investor judgment.

## Decision states
1. ACCUMULATE — thesis strong and valuation provides sufficient margin of safety.
2. HOLD — thesis intact and risk/reward remains acceptable.
3. WATCH — evidence incomplete, valuation unclear, or catalyst not confirmed.
4. REDUCE — thesis weakened, valuation stretched, or portfolio concentration is excessive.
5. EXIT — thesis broken or a critical risk invalidates the original investment case.

## Mandatory gates
- A strong score cannot override a broken thesis.
- Critical risk can cap the final decision at WATCH, REDUCE or EXIT.
- Missing valuation data prevents ACCUMULATE.
- Missing evidence prevents a high-confidence decision.
