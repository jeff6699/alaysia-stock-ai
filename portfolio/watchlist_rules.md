# Watchlist Rules V2.0

## Watchlist entry
Each candidate should contain:
- ticker and company
- investment thesis
- key KPI
- target valuation range
- catalyst
- invalidation condition
- next review date
- evidence quality

## Promotion rule
A watchlist stock may become an active position only when:
1. research coverage is complete,
2. valuation has a defined base case,
3. downside risks are documented,
4. the decision engine returns a non-WATCH result,
5. the investor's position-sizing rules are satisfied.

## No FOMO rule
Price movement alone is never a buy signal.
