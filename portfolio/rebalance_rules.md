# Rebalance Rules V2.0

## Principle
Rebalancing is driven by thesis, valuation, risk and concentration — not short-term price noise.

## Review triggers
- material earnings change
- thesis change
- valuation moves outside target range
- position concentration breach
- major balance-sheet or cash-flow deterioration
- material regulatory or governance event

## Guardrails
- Do not rebalance solely because a stock rose or fell.
- Require an explicit reason and evidence record.
- Preserve the original thesis so changes can be audited.
