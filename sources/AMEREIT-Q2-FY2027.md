## Executive summary

AME REIT reported stronger rental income and net property income (NPI), supported by contributions from acquired properties and tenancy renewals at higher rental rates. Six-month rental income rose to RM34.182 million (+14.6% YoY), while NPI increased to RM31.242 million (+15.3%). Distributable income grew more slowly, by 6.0% to RM23.158 million, and six-month distribution per unit (DPU) increased to 4.34 sen from 4.12 sen.

Reported net income also benefited from RM5.047 million of unrealised fair-value gains on investment properties. Financing costs increased to RM6.688 million from RM4.748 million. Thus, the reported net-income growth should not be confused with equivalent growth in recurring distributable cash income.

The portfolio had 100% occupancy across existing industrial and industrial-related properties, excluding land held for development, according to the manager. The principal monitoring issue is acquisition-led growth alongside increased financing and debt exposure.

## Sequential performance: Q2 FY2027 vs Q1 FY2027

- Revenue increased 5% to RM17.545 million from RM16.637 million.

The manager attributed the sequential increase in revenue, NPI and distributable income mainly to a property acquired near the end of the preceding quarter contributing for a full three months.

Operating cash flow was lower year-on-year despite higher accounting income. Acquisition expenditure and the reliance on financing make cash conversion and incremental returns on acquired assets important follow-up checks.

## Risks and monitoring checklist

1. **Financing-cost pressure:** six-month Islamic financing costs rose approximately 40.9% YoY to RM6.688 million.
2. **Cash-flow conversion:** operating cash flow fell to RM24.727 million from RM36.878 million; monitor cash generated relative to distributable income and distributions.
3. **Acquisition returns:** verify whether the new industrial land and acquired assets generate enough incremental rental income/NPI relative to purchase price and financing cost.
4. **Distribution sustainability:** DPU rose only 5.3% for the half-year while distributable income rose 6.0%; monitor recurring realised income and future distributions.
5. **Fair-value effects:** RM5.047 million of unrealised gains supported accounting profit but is not equivalent to cash income available for distribution.
6. **Occupancy and leasing:** management reports 100% occupancy for existing industrial and industrial-related properties, excluding development land; monitor renewals, tenant concentration and rental reversions.
7. **Related-party exposure:** the report records rental income from companies related to the manager of RM6.198 million for the half-year and acquisition of investment properties from related companies of RM39.300 million. Review transaction terms and governance disclosures.
8. **Unit issuance:** units increased from 531.098 million to 533.024 million, partly due to management fees settled in units; monitor per-unit growth, not just total income.

## Management outlook

The manager was optimistic about favourable performance for the financial year ending 31 March 2027, citing 100% occupancy across existing industrial and industrial-related properties (excluding development land) and its acquisition, asset-management and capital-management strategies. This is management's outlook, not a guarantee.

## Analyst conclusion

**Operationally positive; financing and cash conversion deserve close monitoring.** Rental income and NPI growth are healthy and occupancy is reported at 100% for the relevant existing portfolio. However, distributable income is growing more slowly than reported net income, financing costs are rising, and operating cash flow declined year-on-year. Future reports should demonstrate that acquisition-led growth converts into recurring distributable income per unit and that financing exposure remains manageable.
