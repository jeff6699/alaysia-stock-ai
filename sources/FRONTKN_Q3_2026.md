## Management commentary from source
Management attributed Q3 revenue growth mainly to Taiwan, Malaysia and Singapore subsidiaries. Taiwan semiconductor volume and customer orders were stronger; Singapore benefited from a pick-up in semiconductor volumes; Malaysia's Q3 revenue increased 18% YoY mainly due to oil-and-gas supply-related activities. Management stated that Q3 PAT would have grown 33% using the same foreign-exchange rates as 2025; 9M PAT would have grown 45% on that basis.

Management expects semiconductor activity to remain supported by AI, agentic and generative AI, high-performance computing, automotive electronics and advanced manufacturing. For oil and gas, it cited continued needs for asset reliability, maintenance and operational efficiency, and described its outlook for the remainder of 2026 as cautiously optimistic.
