## Core reported facts

Revenue RM38.16m; gross profit RM10.462m; PBT RM5.597m; PAT RM3.966m; EPS 1.00 sen.

Statement of financial position at 30 June 2026: total assets RM138.074m; total equity RM87.339m; total liabilities RM50.735m; cash and bank balances RM44.506m; total borrowings RM9.595m.

Cash flow for Q1 FY2027: net cash from operating activities RM0.194m; net cash used in investing activities RM0.204m; net cash used in financing activities RM0.935m; cash and cash equivalents at end of period RM42.019m.

Business-segment revenue: solar PV EPCC RM37.449m; sale of electricity RM0.699m; O&M RM0.012m.

The company stated that the higher quarterly revenue was mainly attributable to higher revenue recognised from EPCC of solar PV systems. It also stated that gross profit decreased due to a change in project mix toward relatively lower gross-profit-margin projects.

The company stated that LSS6 comprises a 2,500MW solar quota paired with 1,250MW BESS capacity and that it intends to evaluate and participate in suitable LSS6 bidding opportunities.
