## Key reported facts

- 1QFY27 revenue: **RM38.2 million**, up **36.1% YoY** from RM28.0 million in 1QFY26.
- The quarter represented the Group's **highest quarterly revenue since listing in February 2025**.
- Solar PV EPCC revenue: **RM37.4 million**, up **36.6% YoY**, representing approximately **98.1%** of Group revenue.
- Gross profit: **RM10.5 million** versus RM10.8 million a year earlier.
- Gross profit margin: **27.4%**, down from 38.4%, attributed to a project-mix shift toward larger-scale contracts with comparatively lower percentage margins.
- PBT: **RM5.6 million**, broadly stable YoY.
- PAT: approximately **RM4.0 million**, versus RM3.8 million a year earlier.
- PATAMI: **RM3.97 million**.
- Basic EPS: **1.00 sen**, versus 0.97 sen previously.
- Cash and bank balances: **RM44.5 million**.
- Borrowings: **RM9.6 million**.
- Net cash position: approximately **RM34.9 million**.
- Gearing ratio: **0.11 times**.

## Sequential performance

- 1QFY27 revenue increased **84.1% QoQ** from RM20.7 million in 4QFY26.

## Management / strategy statements

Management stated that the focus is on overall contract economics, disciplined execution and protecting absolute returns rather than pursuing revenue volume for its own sake.

The Group intends to continue building its commercial and industrial distributed-solar activities while selectively pursuing utility-scale opportunities where contract structure and risk-return profile are acceptable.

## LSS6 / BESS

- Malaysia's LSS6 programme was announced in July 2026.
- LSS6 comprises **2,500MW of solar generation capacity paired with 1,250MW of BESS capacity**.
- Northern Solar intends to evaluate suitable LSS6 bidding opportunities, either independently or through strategic partnerships.
- The stated broader expansion areas include commercial and industrial solar, Solar ATAP, BESS and utility-scale solar.

## Main Market transition

The press release states that the Securities Commission Malaysia approved Northern Solar's proposed transfer from the ACE Market to the Main Market of Bursa Malaysia.

## Investment interpretation

The press release confirms strong top-line execution in 1QFY27 but also confirms that percentage gross margins are under pressure as project mix shifts toward larger-scale contracts.

For future investment monitoring, the important validation points are:
1. EPCC gross-margin recovery or stabilization.
2. Cash conversion and working-capital discipline.
3. New EPCC contract wins and order-book replenishment.
4. Execution of existing projects.
5. Progress in LSS6 and BESS opportunities.
6. Utility-scale expansion without excessive balance-sheet or project-risk escalation.

**Important:** The above investment interpretation is research analysis. It is not company guidance and should not be treated as a buy/sell instruction.
