## Key Facts

Aemulus Corporation Sdn Bhd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Aemulus Holdings Berhad, secured new orders on 7 October 2026 with a cumulative value of US$3.86 million, equivalent to RM15,772,732, from a customer in Singapore for delivery of test systems for the Data Centre / AI market.

The orders are expected to be fulfilled within 12 months. Revenue is to be recognised progressively upon delivery, and the orders are expected to contribute positively to FY2027 earnings.

The announcement states that the orders will not affect share capital or the shareholding structure. No direct or indirect interest by directors, major shareholders or connected persons was disclosed.
