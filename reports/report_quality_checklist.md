# Full Research Report Quality Checklist — V1.6

Complete before publishing or treating a company report as decision-ready. Record N/A plus reason where an item cannot be verified.

## Identity, scope and sources
- [ ] Company legal identity, Bursa ticker, exchange/board and sector are correct.
- [ ] Analysis date and current-price date/source are shown.
- [ ] Latest available financial period is identified, with publication and audited/reviewed/unaudited status.
- [ ] Units, currency, consolidation basis, fiscal periods and share basis are consistent.
- [ ] Every material financial figure and factual claim has a source and locator.
- [ ] Issuer statements are distinguished from independent evidence; source dates and caveats are visible.

## Evidence and analysis
- [ ] Facts are separated from ANALYSIS, ASSUMPTION, INFERENCE and FORECAST.
- [ ] No fabricated or unsupported financial/company/market information appears.
- [ ] Missing data are marked N/A with their effect on confidence/coverage.
- [ ] Financial history covers five years where available; a shorter history is explained.
- [ ] Industry analysis covers growth, cycle, structure, competition, pricing, barriers, regulation, technology/AI, demand drivers, tailwinds, headwinds, risks and opportunities.
- [ ] Segment, earnings-quality and cash-flow claims reconcile to disclosed information or are marked N/A.
- [ ] Company-specific forecasts and valuation assumptions are explicitly identified as assumptions.

## Upstream framework integrity
- [ ] V1.2 quality score, rating, coverage, strengths and weaknesses trace to the unchanged V1.2 modules.
- [ ] V1.3 moat score, durability, evolution, AI disruption risk and opportunity trace to unchanged V1.3 modules.
- [ ] V1.4 price/value range, method selection, MOS, confidence and assumptions trace to unchanged V1.4 modules.
- [ ] V1.5 risks, catalysts, scenarios, data confidence, gates, overrides and final decision trace to unchanged V1.5 modules.
- [ ] No V1.2/V1.3/V1.4/V1.5 methodology, weight, threshold or formula has been edited by the V1.6 report process.
- [ ] Audit trail names module version, source date and any transformation.

## Risk, catalysts, scenarios and decision
- [ ] All 11 V1.5 risk dimensions were assessed, with N/A and effect where evidence is unavailable.
- [ ] Top five risks are ranked with impact, probability and time horizon; evidence, mitigation and monitoring signal are included.
- [ ] Catalysts are evidence-based, classified near/medium/long term, and include timeframe, probability, impact, evidence and confirmation signal.
- [ ] Bull/Base/Bear probabilities are explicit analyst assumptions and sum exactly to 100%.
- [ ] Scenario values trace to V1.4; expected returns use consistent price/value/share basis; missing values are N/A.
- [ ] V1.5 decision score, coverage, confidence, override/gates and final classification are traceable and internally consistent.
- [ ] The decision explanation states why the rating applies and what could change it.

## Thesis, monitoring and presentation
- [ ] Thesis says why company could win and fail, what must go right, and what invalidates it.
- [ ] Monitoring checklist covers quarterly revenue, quarterly EPS, margins, FCF, debt, cash, ROE/ROIC, new contracts, order book, capacity, industry, AI, competitors, management actions and valuation.
- [ ] Each indicator has what to monitor, why, positive signal, negative signal, frequency and source where available.
- [ ] Overall data confidence is HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW and supported by completeness, recency, source quality, consistency and unresolved assumptions.
- [ ] All 20 master report sections are present; dates, table headings and terminology are consistent.
- [ ] Disclaimer is included and makes clear this is not personalized advice or a precise prediction.
