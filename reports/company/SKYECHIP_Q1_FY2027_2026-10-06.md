## 1. Executive Summary

SkyeChip delivered **RM49.62m revenue** and **RM13.92m profit after tax (PAT)** in 1Q FY2027. Gross profit was RM20.82m and profit before tax (PBT) was RM14.43m. There is no year-on-year comparable interim quarter because this is only the Group's second interim financial report.

The key issue is that 1Q FY2027 was materially weaker than the immediately preceding quarter: revenue fell **15.7%** from RM58.88m to RM49.62m, while PBT fell **54.9%** and PAT fell **55.8%**. Management attributes the revenue decline mainly to the timing of project milestones, with silicon IP revenue lower by about RM7.7m. Excluding the **RM2.53m one-off IPO expense**, PBT would have been about RM17.0m and PAT about RM16.4m.

Despite the quarterly decline, the balance sheet became substantially stronger after the IPO. Total equity rose to **RM558.9m**, cash and bank balances were **RM105.2m**, while total borrowings were only **RM5.35m**. The Group also held **RM330.3m of other investments** at 30 June 2026.

## 4. Sequential Performance

Compared with 4Q FY2026:

- Revenue: RM58.875m → RM49.623m, **-15.7%**
- PBT: RM32.040m → RM14.425m, **-54.9%**
- PAT: RM31.469m → RM13.915m, **-55.8%**

Management says the revenue decline was mainly due to **timing of project milestones**, particularly lower silicon IP contribution. Higher employee benefit expenses and the one-off IPO expense also pressured profitability.

On an adjusted basis excluding the IPO expense:

- Adjusted PBT: ~RM17.0m
- Adjusted PAT: ~RM16.4m

The quarter therefore shows **earnings volatility**, rather than enough evidence yet to conclude that the underlying business has structurally deteriorated.

This means the **large majority of IPO funds remain available** for future R&D, capacity and infrastructure expansion.

## 8. Strategic Catalysts

Management identifies long-term growth drivers in:

- Artificial intelligence
- High-performance computing
- Automotive applications

The company aims to benefit through its **silicon IP and custom ASIC design** capabilities.

A potentially important strategic development is the ARM programme. SkyeChip applied for access to an **ARM Compute Subsystem (CSS) platform** and an **ARM Flexible Access (AFA) platform**. It received conditional approvals from MIDA in April 2026 and entered into a definitive agreement in June 2026 for access to the AFA token. The company was still evaluating the terms for the CSS token at the reporting date.

The company also incorporated a wholly-owned US subsidiary, **SkyeChip Inc.**, on 15 June 2026 for RM609,750.

## 10. Key Risks / What to Watch

### Earnings volatility
The sharp sequential decline shows that project milestone timing can materially affect quarterly revenue and profit.

### Customer/geographical concentration
Around **96.7% of revenue came from the USA and China**, creating concentration and geopolitical/export-control sensitivity.

### Project timing risk
Silicon IP revenue fell about RM7.7m sequentially mainly because of project milestone timing. Future quarters should be monitored for conversion of project pipeline into revenue.

### IPO fund deployment
Only 2.9% of IPO proceeds had been utilised by quarter-end. The important question is whether the remaining capital can generate attractive incremental revenue, margins and ROIC.

### Dilution
The company offered **61.755m ESOS options** with an exercise price of RM0.88 and vesting of up to five years. Diluted EPS was already 0.90 sen versus basic EPS of 0.93 sen.

### Valuation risk
The report itself does not provide a market-price valuation. A proper investment decision therefore requires checking the current share price and calculating forward P/E, EV/EBITDA and/or FCF-based valuation separately.

## Bottom Line

**1Q FY2027 is fundamentally healthy but not a strong sequential quarter.** The biggest positives are high margins, low debt, positive operating cash flow and a very strong post-IPO balance sheet. The biggest issue is earnings visibility: revenue and PAT fell sharply QoQ because of project milestone timing.

For a long-term AI/semiconductor investment thesis, the next major confirmation should be **recovery in Silicon IP revenue + continued Custom ASIC growth + productive deployment of the RM352m IPO capital**. Until those are demonstrated, the stock should be treated as a **high-potential but higher-execution-risk growth candidate** rather than a fully proven compounder.
