# SC Estate Builder Berhad (SCBUILD / 0109) — Q2 FY2027 Investment Summary

> Source: `SCBuild - Q2 2027 Results.pdf` (unaudited quarterly report for the quarter ended 31 July 2026)
> Archived in: `reports/company/`

## 1. Executive Summary

**Investment classification: 转型观察型（Transformation Watch）**

SCBUILD Q2 FY2027 shows strong revenue growth but weaker pre-tax profit. The business mix is changing materially: building-material trading has become the main revenue and profit contributor, while the traditional construction segment remains weak. The company also has a sizeable pipeline/order-book story involving construction, solar/renewable energy and property development.

The key issue for investors is **cash-flow quality**: trade receivables rose sharply while cash fell to only RM0.497 million.

### Core thesis

**订单 → 收入 → 毛利 → 净利 → 现金流**

The next few quarters should be used to verify whether SCBUILD can turn its reported projects and order book into sustainable earnings and cash flow.

- Capacity: **4.00 MW**

> Important: project value / GDV should **not** be treated as future profit. Actual earnings depend on execution, margins, recognition timing and cash collection.

## 6. Strategic Expansion

### Sentosa Club Hotel

SCBUILD announced the proposed acquisition of **25% equity interest** in Sentosa Club Hotel Sdn. Bhd.

- Purchase consideration: **RM18.791m**
- Consideration: redeemable convertible preference shares (RCPS)

Management's stated objectives include diversification into property investment, enhancing long-term earnings potential and establishing the group's future headquarters within the property.

## 7. Bull Case

1. Building-material trading continues to scale and becomes a stable profit contributor.
2. Construction project execution accelerates.
3. Large project pipeline converts into recognised revenue and earnings.
4. Solar / renewable-energy projects become a meaningful recurring earnings stream.
5. Property development and investment provide additional long-term value.

---

## 8. Bear Case / Risks

1. **Profit growth lags revenue growth.**
2. Traditional construction segment remains loss-making.
3. **Receivables increased sharply**, creating collection and working-capital risk.
4. **Cash only RM0.497m**, leaving limited liquidity.
5. Large GDV/order-book numbers may take years to translate into earnings.
6. Business diversification increases execution complexity.
7. The RCPS-based property acquisition needs to be monitored for dilution/financing implications.
