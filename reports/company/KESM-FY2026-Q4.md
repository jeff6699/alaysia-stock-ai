# KESM Industries Berhad (9334) — FY2026 Q4 / Full Year Results

## Executive Summary
FY2026 was a turnaround year. Revenue increased 6% to RM222.8m and the Group moved from FY2025 pre-tax loss of RM7.5m to FY2026 pre-tax profit of RM12.9m. The company stated that the improvement was mainly driven by higher demand for services processing AI-related chips.

4QFY2026 revenue was RM61.5m (+15% YoY) and PBT RM3.8m, compared with RM2.4m in 3QFY2026.

## Key Financials
- FY2026 Revenue: RM222.8m (+6%)
- FY2026 PBT: RM12.9m, turnaround from RM7.5m loss
- FY2026 Net Profit: RM9.5m
- FY2026 EPS: 22.0 sen
- 4QFY2026 Revenue: RM61.5m (+15% YoY)
- 4QFY2026 PBT: RM3.8m

## AI / Semiconductor
Management highlighted higher demand for burn-in and testing services for AI-related chips. The Group expects structural growth from AI and the digital economy and continues investing in quality systems, operational excellence and productivity to handle more complex AI-chip requirements.

## Cash Flow / Balance Sheet
- Operating cash flow: RM51.1m
- PPE: RM161.5m (+21%)
- Cash and short-term deposits: RM217.4m
- Cash and cash equivalents: RM83.8m
- Total borrowings: RM41.0m
- FY2026 PPE cash purchases: RM52.4m
- Additional committed PPE purchases: RM20.2m

## Earnings Quality
FY2026 other income increased RM9.9m. This included machinery/test-equipment disposal gains, RM1.2m compensation for unfulfilled minimum orders and RM1.0m income from accelerated deliveries. Therefore the recurring earnings recovery still needs to be confirmed by subsequent quarters.

## Dividend
FY2026 interim dividends totalled 10.5 sen/share:
- First interim: 6.0 sen
- Second interim: 4.5 sen
- No final dividend recommended.

## Investment Research View
Status: TURNAROUND + AI DEMAND RECOVERY

KESM is becoming more interesting as an AI semiconductor infrastructure/services play, but FY2026 does not yet prove that it is a high-margin AI compounder.

### Bull Case
AI chip demand continues rising, improving testing/burn-in utilisation and operating leverage.

### Base Case
AI demand supports moderate revenue growth, but capex and depreciation absorb part of the earnings improvement.

### Bear Case
AI capacity shifts create uneven loading while capex/depreciation rise faster than revenue.

### Next Quarter Watchlist
1. Core operating profit excluding non-recurring/other-income effects
2. Quarterly margin expansion
3. Utilisation of new testing equipment
4. Sustainability of AI-related demand
5. Operating cash flow after capex
6. ROE/ROIC improvement
