# RAMSSOL Group Berhad (0236) — Q2 2026 Research Archive

## Source
- Unaudited Interim Financial Report for the second quarter ended 30 June 2026
- FY ending: 31 December 2026
- Board approval: 7 August 2026
- Source: Bursa Malaysia filing

## 1. Executive Summary
RAMSSOL delivered another quarter of growth in Q2 2026. Revenue rose 9.41% YoY to RM27.28m, while profit attributable to owners (PATAMI) increased 29.90% to RM7.43m. For 6M2026, revenue grew 14.95% to RM49.48m and PATAMI grew 20.50% to RM13.93m.

The main operating drivers cited by management were stronger enterprise AiTech deployments in Indonesia, PeopleTech CaaS subscriptions, and scaling AutoTech motorcycle transactions. Sequentially, Q2 revenue increased 22.88% from Q1 and PATAMI increased 14.31%.

## 6. Strategic Catalysts
### AiTech
Management expects enterprise adoption of generative AI, smart automation and cloud deployment to support higher-value engagements and potentially improve margin mix.

### PeopleTech
CaaS and talent solutions are expected to be the most immediate scaling contributor. The acquisition of INNIO Group is intended to broaden the client base and headcount under management.

### AutoTech
Motorcycle trading platform and JPJ collection agency operations continue to scale.

### FinTech
PayDayNow Earned Wage Access (EWA) integration with AmBank was formalised through a 20 July 2026 MOA.

### INNIO acquisition
RAMS Fintech acquired 60% of INNIO Holding and 49% of Agensi Pekerjaan INNIO for total consideration of RM14.40m: RM3.60m cash plus RM10.80m via 15.428m new RAMSSOL shares at RM0.70/share. Completion occurred on 24 July 2026.

## 7. Risks / Watch Items
1. Non-controlling interest increased sharply: RM1.65m in Q2 and RM3.09m in 6M2026, meaning a growing portion of group profit is not attributable to RAMSSOL shareholders.
2. Share count increased; weighted average shares rose to 404.16m in 6M2026 from 371.63m in 6M2025. Diluted EPS was 3.10 sen versus 2.32 sen.
3. Intangible asset amortisation/adjustment materially reduced retained earnings/equity.
4. Future growth increasingly depends on successful scaling of AiTech, PeopleTech, AutoTech and FinTech initiatives.
5. INNIO acquisition creates execution and integration risk; contribution should be tracked from Q3 onward.
6. No dividend was declared for Q2 2026.

## 8. Investment View — Research Stage
**Current signal: Positive operational momentum, but valuation and dilution need to be checked before an investment conclusion.**

Positive:
- Double-digit 6M growth in revenue, operating profit and PBT.
- PATAMI growth of 20.5% YTD.
- Strong operating cash flow.
- Low reported net debt relative to cash.
- Multiple growth pillars and regional expansion.
- AiTech provides exposure to enterprise AI adoption.

Caution:
- NCI is rising.
- Share dilution is material.
- Intangible asset adjustment is large.
- Acquisition execution remains unproven in reported results.
- The report alone does not provide enough information to determine whether the current share price offers a sufficient margin of safety.
