## 7. Main Risks

### A. Cash flow risk
Profit has improved, but operating cash flow remains negative. The next major confirmation is whether earnings can convert into cash.

### B. Leverage
Borrowings remain high relative to equity and cash. Finance costs were **RM3.62m** in 1H2026, limiting the conversion of operating profit into bottom-line earnings.

### C. Thin margins
Despite strong YoY improvement, 1H net margin remained only about 1.4%. A small deterioration in gross/operating margin could have a meaningful effect on earnings.

### D. Working-capital intensity
Receivables and inventories are large relative to revenue, making working-capital discipline critical.

## 8. Forward Outlook

### Base case — Improving but still fragile
If revenue continues growing and the improved operating profitability is sustained, ASTEEL could continue its earnings recovery. The company is moving in the right direction, but the quality of the recovery still needs confirmation from cash flow.

### Bull case
A sustained improvement in margins plus better working-capital collection could create significant earnings leverage because the current profit base is small. In that scenario, EPS could improve much faster than revenue.

### Bear case
If revenue growth slows, margins reverse, or receivables/inventory remain elevated, the combination of thin margins, negative operating cash flow and high finance costs could pressure earnings and liquidity.
