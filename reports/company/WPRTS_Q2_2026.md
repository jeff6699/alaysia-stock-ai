# WPRTS (Westports Holdings Berhad) — 2Q FY2026 / 6M2026

> Source: Quarterly Financial Report for the financial period ended 30 June 2026.

## 1. Executive Summary

- 2Q2026 operational revenue: RM808.2m, +33% YoY.
- 2Q2026 PBT: RM471.3m, +57% YoY.
- 2Q2026 PAT: RM360.9m, +56% YoY.
- 6M2026 operational revenue: RM1.561bn, +30% YoY.
- 6M2026 PBT: RM897.8m, +51% YoY.
- 6M2026 PAT: RM687.4m, +51% YoY.
- 6M2026 EPS: 20.04 sen vs 13.32 sen, +50%.
- Main earnings driver: tariff increase, stronger container/VAS revenue and higher container volume in 2Q.
- 2Q vs 1Q2026: operational revenue +7%, PBT +10%, PAT +11%.
- 2026 management expects overall container throughput to be roughly similar to the previous year.

## 2. Financial Quality

### Profitability
- 6M gross profit: RM1.099bn, +41% YoY.
- 6M PBT: RM897.8m, +51%.
- 6M PAT: RM687.4m, +51%.
- PAT margin on reported revenue is about 39%; the business remains highly cash-generative.

### Cash Flow
- 6M operating cash flow: RM998.6m vs RM716.5m, +39%.
- 6M investing cash outflow: RM360.3m, mainly concession assets and PPE investment.
- Cash and short-term investments: RM1.130bn at 30 June 2026.

### Balance Sheet
- Total equity: RM4.522bn.
- Total borrowings: RM1.030bn.
- Concession assets: RM5.939bn.
- Balance sheet remains manageable relative to operating cash generation, but expansion-related borrowing is rising.

## 3. Expansion / Long-Term Growth

Westports is progressing with the proposed CT10–CT17 expansion. The concession period for CT10–CT13 has been extended to 31 August 2070; a further extension to 2082 is linked to the required underwater-land acquisition/transfer for CT14–CT17.

- Dredging and land reclamation started in 2025.
- Construction is expected to take about four years.
- CT10 is expected to be ready by 2028.
- Authorised and contracted capex commitments at 30 June 2026: RM934.6m.
- RM5bn SWMTN programme established to support the expansion.

This is the key long-term growth asset: near-term earnings are already strong, while the expansion can support a much longer concession runway and future capacity.

## 4. Dividend

- First interim dividend for FY2026: 14.98 sen/share.
- Cash portion: 11.99 sen.
- Electable DRP portion: 2.99 sen.
- This compares with 9.93 sen first interim dividend for the corresponding period of FY2025.

Dividend remains an important part of the investment thesis.

## 5. Risks / Watchlist

1. Container throughput: management currently expects 2026 throughput to be roughly flat YoY; therefore future earnings growth may rely more on tariff/VAS and operational efficiency than volume growth.
2. Expansion capex: CT10–CT17 requires substantial capital expenditure and has increased borrowings.
3. Interest cost: 6M finance costs rose to RM66.6m from RM57.6m.
4. Geopolitical/shipping-route risk: regional transhipment volume depends on shipping-line decisions, supply chains and regional economic conditions.
5. LBT 4/4A incident: temporary closure occurred after a January 2026 vessel incident; partial reopening happened in February. Management does not expect a material earnings impact; insurance and salvage work remain ongoing.
6. Valuation risk: strong earnings can already be reflected in the share price. Investment attractiveness should therefore be assessed against current market price and forward yield/PE rather than earnings growth alone.

## 6. Investment Outlook

### Overall view: Positive / Defensive Growth

WPRTS shows an unusually strong combination of:
- high recurring operating cash flow;
- strong pricing/tariff power;
- long concession duration;
- rising VAS contribution;
- strong dividend profile;
- major expansion runway toward CT10–CT17.

The main limitation is that management itself does not expect container throughput to grow materially in 2026. Therefore, the next stage of the thesis is less about explosive volume growth and more about tariff monetisation, VAS growth, disciplined capex and successful expansion execution.

### 3-Year Thesis
**2026:** earnings benefit from tariff increases and VAS; monitor throughput and cash flow.

**2027:** watch whether tariff/VAS gains continue to offset flat container volumes and rising financing costs.

**2028+:** CT10 becoming operational is the major potential capacity-growth catalyst.
