# Hartanah Kenyalang Berhad (HKB)

## Q3 FY2026 Financial Results
**Reporting period:** Quarter ended 31 July 2026  
**Source:** Interim Financial Report Q3 FY2026

### 1. Financial highlights

| Metric | Q3 FY2026 | Q3 FY2025 | 9M FY2026 | 9M FY2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | RM49.54m | RM25.62m | RM117.85m | RM100.48m |
| Profit before tax | RM4.21m | -RM1.74m | RM7.82m | RM2.85m |
| Profit after tax | RM3.04m | -RM1.70m | RM5.71m | RM1.52m |
| EPS | 0.48 sen | -0.27 sen | 0.90 sen | 0.25 sen |

### 2. Key takeaways

- Q3 revenue increased **93.4% YoY**, mainly driven by the Stadium Sarawak Project.
- Q3 turned profitable, with PBT of **RM4.21m** versus a loss of RM1.74m previously.
- 9M revenue grew **17.3% YoY**, while PBT rose **174.4%**.
- Building construction became the main revenue contributor: **RM87.32m / 74%** of 9M revenue.
- Infrastructure construction contributed **RM30.53m / 26%**.

### 3. Order/project catalysts

- Stadium Sarawak Project: 1-year contract commencing January 2026.
- Wisma JKR Sarawak Project: described by the company as its largest project by contract value to date.
- First design-and-build project: new prison in Sibu with capacity for 1,000 inmates.
- Expansion into West Malaysia through Hartanah Kenari Sdn Bhd; first project is construction of three 4-storey factories.
- Management plans to expand into utilities construction, including electrical substations, water treatment plants and water pipe laying.

### 4. Balance sheet / cash flow

- Cash and bank balances: **RM40.70m**
- Fixed deposits: **RM11.16m**
- Total borrowings: **RM25.07m**, up from RM8.25m at 31 Oct 2025.
- 9M operating cash flow: **RM1.69m**
- 9M net profit: **RM5.71m**
- Contract assets increased to **RM67.91m** from RM38.36m.

**Main watchpoint:** profit growth has not yet translated fully into operating cash flow, while contract assets and borrowings have increased.

### 5. Capital raising

Private placement completed on 29 May 2026:
- 62m new shares issued.
- Gross proceeds: **RM17.05m**.
- Solarvest subscribed for 45m shares, representing **6.6%** of enlarged share capital.
- Norges Bank subscribed for the remaining 17m shares.
- As at 31 July 2026, RM10.255m had been utilised and **RM6.795m remained** for project working capital.

### 6. Investment view

**Fundamental trend: Improving**

**Positive**
- Strong Q3 revenue acceleration.
- Significant PBT growth.
- New Sarawak projects provide near-term revenue visibility.
- Expansion into design & build and West Malaysia.
- Healthy cash balance after fund raising.

**Risks / watchpoints**
- Operating cash flow remains low relative to accounting profit.
- Contract assets are rising sharply.
- Borrowings have increased materially.
- Earnings may be sensitive to project execution and completion timing.
- Share count increased following private placement.

4. Contract assets conversion into receivables and cash.
