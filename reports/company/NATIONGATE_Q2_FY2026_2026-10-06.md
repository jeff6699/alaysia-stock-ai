# NATIONGATE HOLDINGS BERHAD (0270) — 2Q FY2026 Results

- **Reporting period:** Second quarter ended 30 June 2026
- **Report type:** Unaudited Interim Financial Report
- **Source:** User-provided NHB Q2 2026.pdf
- **Repository:** `reports/company/`

## 1. Executive Summary

NationGate recorded **RM1.769bn revenue**, **RM58.4m gross profit**, **RM8.1m PBT** and **RM11.1m PAT** in 2Q FY2026. Compared with 2Q FY2025, revenue fell 32.1%, PBT fell 90.3% and PAT fell 81.4%, mainly because of a sharp reduction in the Data Computing segment.

The important positive signal is the **sequential recovery**: revenue increased 2.8% QoQ, gross profit more than doubled (+106.5%), PBT recovered from a **RM8.45m loss in 1Q FY2026 to RM8.07m profit**, and PAT improved from a **RM7.74m loss to RM11.05m profit**. Management attributed the improvement mainly to stronger Networking & Telecommunications revenue and better Data Computing performance.

For 1H FY2026, however, the Group remained almost at break-even: revenue was **RM3.489bn**, PBT was a **RM0.37m loss**, and PAT was **RM3.31m** versus RM112.85m PAT in 1H FY2025.

## 3. Sequential Recovery

Compared with 1Q FY2026:

- Revenue: RM1.720bn → **RM1.769bn**, +2.8%
- Gross profit: RM28.3m → **RM58.4m**, +106.5%
- PBT: RM8.45m loss → **RM8.07m profit**
- PAT: RM7.74m loss → **RM11.05m profit**
- Owners' PAT: RM0.51m → **RM10.90m**

This is the strongest part of the quarter. The business has moved from a loss in 1Q to profit in 2Q, although earnings are still far below the prior-year level.

## 7. Cash Flow — Main Warning Signal

1H FY2026 cash flow from operations was **negative RM97.9m**, although this was a major improvement from negative RM1.084bn in 1H FY2025.

Key working-capital movements:
- Inventory released **RM461.1m** of cash
- Receivables absorbed **RM743.5m**
- Payables provided **RM144.6m**

Investing cash flow was **negative RM88.4m**, including **RM93.8m capex**.

Financing cash flow was **positive RM89.8m**, mainly supported by additional borrowings.

Closing cash fell from RM417.5m at 31 December 2025 to **RM321.1m**.

**Interpretation:** cash-flow pressure has improved substantially year-on-year, but the Group has not yet demonstrated consistently positive operating cash generation.

## 8. Capex & Expansion

Approved and contracted property, plant and equipment commitments at 30 June 2026 were **RM362.8m**, versus RM98.5m a year earlier.

This is a significant increase and indicates continued capacity/manufacturing investment.

The report also states that the Group is advancing **photonics-related initiatives**, with related facilities expected to be completed by **4Q 2026**.

## 9. AI / Data Centre / Photonics Thesis

Management explicitly links its outlook to:

- Artificial intelligence
- Cloud computing
- Digital infrastructure
- Server-related manufacturing
- High-speed data transmission
- Optical connectivity
- Photonics

The strategic thesis is therefore broader than conventional EMS: NationGate is attempting to increase participation in **higher-value technology manufacturing and photonics**.

Management states that Malaysia's established E&E ecosystem supports this expansion.

## 10. Prospects

Management is **cautiously optimistic** for FY2026.

The main growth drivers identified are continued AI, cloud and digital-infrastructure adoption, which should support server manufacturing and advanced technology solutions. Rapid AI workloads are also expected to increase demand for high-speed data transmission and optical connectivity.

The key near-term milestone is the **photonics facility expected to be completed in 4Q 2026**.

## 12. Key Risks

### Data Computing concentration
Around 85–87% of revenue still comes from Data Computing. A slowdown in this segment can materially affect Group earnings.

### Earnings recovery not yet proven
Although 2Q returned to profit, 1H PBT remains negative and 1H PAT is down about 97% YoY.

### Thin margins
2Q gross margin was only about 3.3% and PBT margin about 0.46%. Small changes in costs, FX or utilisation can have a large effect on earnings.

### Balance-sheet leverage
Borrowings reached RM901.9m and gearing increased to 0.88x.

### Cash conversion
Operating cash flow remained negative in 1H FY2026, while trade receivables reached RM1.52bn.

### Capex execution
Approved/contracted PPE commitments of RM362.8m are large relative to current profitability. Returns on this investment need to be monitored.

### FX sensitivity
The Group recorded substantial unrealised FX effects and uses USD forward contracts. The notional value of FX hedging contracts was RM2.238bn (USD548m equivalent).

## 15. Bottom Line

**NationGate is showing the first signs of a turnaround, but the turnaround is not yet confirmed.**

The most encouraging signal is the sharp QoQ recovery: 2Q FY2026 returned to profit and gross profit more than doubled. The structural AI/data-centre/photonics story is also attractive.

However, the investment case still carries meaningful execution risk because **Data Computing dominates revenue, margins are thin, gearing is 0.88x, and operating cash flow remains negative.**

For a long-term investor, the next confirmation should be:

**Data Computing stabilises → gross margin improves → PBT/PAT recover → operating cash flow turns sustainably positive → photonics begins contributing.**

If these five conditions occur together, NationGate's investment thesis becomes materially stronger. Until then, it should be classified as a **high-growth / turnaround candidate requiring quarterly monitoring**, rather than a proven compounder.
