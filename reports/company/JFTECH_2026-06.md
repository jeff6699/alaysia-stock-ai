## Executive summary
JF Technology delivered a record quarter and record 12-month period. Q4 revenue reached RM27.40m, PBT RM6.32m and parent-attributable net profit RM4.84m. Revenue increased 18.2% QoQ, PBT 38.4% QoQ and parent net profit 51.5% QoQ. The company reported its fifth consecutive quarter of revenue growth.

For the 12 months ended 30 June 2026, revenue was RM86.66m, PBT RM16.37m and parent-attributable net profit RM12.03m. The company says these are record levels.

## Business / demand
Management attributed the record quarter to healthier sales across test contacting solutions, test interface products, the Kunshan manufacturing facility, probe cards and test-socket laser cleaners.

Management states that order visibility across key business segments remains healthy and that probe-card and test-socket laser-cleaner operations are performing well.

The company is positioned in semiconductor test contacting and interface solutions, early in the semiconductor value chain. Management sees AI, data-centre investment, advanced computing, 5G/6G, healthcare and defence as demand drivers.

## Cash flow
12M operating cash flow was negative RM10.05m despite RM16.37m PBT. Main working-capital uses included:
- Receivables increase: RM16.47m
- Inventory increase: RM3.54m
- Payables decrease: RM10.99m

Investing cash flow was negative RM5.18m, including RM5.51m investment in an associate.

Financing cash flow was positive RM9.59m, mainly helped by RM19.13m net proceeds from share issuance.

This means earnings quality is currently good on profitability but cash conversion needs monitoring.

## Corporate actions / M&A
The Group acquired 100% equity interest in Transcend Technologies (S) Pte. Ltd. and Transcend Tech Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd., which holds 95.5% of Transcend Technologies Inc., in December 2025.

Goodwill increased from RM4.50m to RM21.56m, and investments in associates increased from RM1.02m to RM6.91m. The acquisition therefore materially changed the group structure and should be monitored for earnings contribution and integration benefits.

The company also issued 42.971m shares through private placement for RM19.337m. By the report date, RM18.662m remained unutilised, with the majority intended for capital expenditure within 60 months.

## Dividend
Two interim dividends of 0.25 sen per share were paid during the period, one in December 2025 and one in June 2026. No dividend was declared for the current quarter.

## Key risks / watchlist
1. Working-capital pressure: receivables and inventory rose while operating cash flow was negative.
2. Acquisition execution: Transcend-related goodwill rose materially; future returns from the acquisition need to justify the investment.
3. Semiconductor cyclicality: current results are benefiting from a strong cycle.
4. Geopolitical/supply-chain risk: management highlighted persistent geopolitical tensions and elevated input costs.
5. Tax normalisation: a subsidiary's 100% Pioneer Status tax exemption expired on 31 March 2026.
6. Share dilution: private placement increased the share base, although the proceeds strengthen funding capacity.
7. Customer/geographic concentration: China, Malaysia and wider Asia are major revenue contributors.

## Investment outlook
### Bull case
AI/data-centre semiconductor demand remains strong, test-interface demand continues to expand, probe-card and laser-cleaner businesses scale, and Transcend contributes meaningfully. Record quarterly revenue and PBT could become a new earnings base.

### Base case
The semiconductor upcycle remains supportive, but growth normalises from the current record pace. Healthy order visibility supports continued sequential earnings growth, while working-capital conversion and acquisition integration determine the quality of the next leg.

### Bear case
Semiconductor capex slows, customer orders weaken, receivables remain elevated, and acquisition returns disappoint. In that scenario, current record earnings may represent a cycle peak rather than a sustainable base.

## Bottom line
JFTECH's operating momentum is clearly positive. The strongest signals are five consecutive quarters of revenue growth, record Q4 revenue/PBT, improving QoQ profitability, low debt and RM55m cash.

The main issue is not demand visibility but earnings-to-cash conversion and whether the Transcend acquisition can convert into durable earnings growth.

Investment view from this report alone: **Positive / Growth Watchlist**, but not a blind buy. The next 2–3 reporting periods should focus on (1) revenue growth, (2) PBT/net-profit growth, (3) operating cash flow turning positive, (4) receivables normalising, (5) Transcend contribution, and (6) utilisation of the RM18.66m placement proceeds.
