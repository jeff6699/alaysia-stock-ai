# Critical Holdings Berhad — FY2026 Q4 Summary

> Source: Critical Holdings Berhad Interim Financial Report, fourth quarter ended 30 June 2026, dated 26 August 2026. The report is unaudited.

## 1. Executive summary

**核心结论：基本面出现“Q4回升 + FY全年承压 + 订单强劲”的组合。**

- Q4 FY2026 revenue RM102.66m, down 6.86% YoY, but gross profit RM15.93m, up from RM13.51m.
- Q4 PBT RM11.59m was slightly above Q4 FY2025 RM11.19m; PAT RM8.16m was slightly below RM8.30m because of higher tax expense.
- FY2026 revenue fell 7.03% to RM305.79m; PAT fell about 20% to RM22.33m.
- Q4 gross margin improved to **15.52%** from **12.25%** in Q4 FY2025 and from 10.93% in Q3 FY2026.
- Order book was approximately **RM1.016bn**, more than 3x FY2026 revenue, and the company secured a record **RM772m** contract.
- Balance sheet remained lightly levered: cash/bank balances RM73.16m versus borrowings RM2.12m.
- Main concern: FY2026 operating cash flow dropped sharply to RM8.70m from RM38.58m while capex rose to RM14.28m.

## 3. Sequential momentum

Against Q3 FY2026:
- Revenue: RM50.81m → RM102.66m (**+102.05%**)
- Gross profit: RM5.55m → RM15.93m
- Gross margin: 10.93% → **15.52%**
- PBT: RM2.00m → RM11.59m
- PAT: RM1.20m → RM8.16m

The company attributes the improvement mainly to higher progress recognition from ongoing MEP engineering projects, together with project mix, completion stages and timing of project-cost recognition.

## 4. Business mix

MEP Engineering Solutions remained dominant at **94.45%** of FY2026 revenue. Maintenance and services contributed **5.55%**.

For Q4 FY2026, project revenue was concentrated in:
1. Cleanroom & process utilities: 42.50%
2. Data centres: 28.55%
3. Plantroom MEP: 25.72%
4. Maintenance/services: 3.23%

This leaves the company highly exposed to the timing and execution of large engineering projects.

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow

**Positive**
- Cash/bank balances: RM73.16m
- Borrowings: RM2.12m
- Approximate cash less borrowings: RM71.04m
- Equity: RM104.44m

**Watch**
- Trade/other receivables: RM93.22m
- Trade/other payables: RM110.21m
- FY2026 operating cash flow: only RM8.70m versus RM38.58m in FY2025
- PPE purchases: RM14.28m

The key issue is therefore not leverage, but **working-capital conversion and execution/cash conversion** as the business scales.

## 6. Order book and catalysts

The report states an order book of approximately **RM1.016bn** and highlights a record **RM772m contract**.

Management views this as a strategic inflection point, with the business moving from a primarily MEP engineering firm toward an integrated **EPCC** solution provider.

Potential demand drivers identified by management include:
- semiconductor and advanced manufacturing capacity expansion;
- cleanroom/process utility infrastructure;
- data-centre development driven by cloud computing, AI and digital services;
- Malaysia's NIMP 2030 and digital-economy initiatives.

## 7. Risks / items to monitor

1. **Execution risk:** RM1.016bn order book creates substantial delivery requirements.
2. **Margin risk:** FY2026 gross margin declined despite the strong Q4 rebound.
3. **Cash conversion:** operating cash flow fell sharply while capex increased.
4. **Project concentration:** engineering solutions account for 94.45% of FY2026 revenue.
5. **Tax:** FY2026 effective tax rate was 26.46%, above the 24% statutory rate.
6. **Order-book monetisation:** the large order book must translate into recognised revenue and cash flow.
7. **Dividend:** no dividend was declared for Q4 FY2026.

## 8. Investment-research status
