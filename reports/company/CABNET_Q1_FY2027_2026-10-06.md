# CABNET Holdings Berhad — FY2027 Q1 Results
**Period:** 3 months ended 31 May 2026  
**Source:** Company unaudited interim financial statements  
**Archived:** 2026-10-06

## 1. Executive Summary
Cabnet Holdings Berhad FY2027 Q1 delivered very strong top-line growth, but earnings growth lagged revenue growth.

- Revenue: RM39.645m, +116.6% YoY
- Gross profit: RM3.097m, +35.3% YoY
- Profit before tax: RM1.123m, +49.3% YoY
- PAT attributable to owners: RM0.565m, -11.4% YoY
- EPS: 0.32 sen vs 0.36 sen
- Operating cash flow: +RM1.253m vs -RM2.615m YoY
- Cash and cash equivalents reported: RM23.814m; cash after pledged/earmarked balances and overdraft: RM6.869m
- Net assets/share: RM0.1928

**Investment view:** Neutral-to-cautiously positive. The business is showing strong revenue momentum and improved operating cash generation, but low margins, high tax burden and working-capital requirements remain the key constraints.

## 2. Financial Performance
| Metric | FY27 Q1 | FY26 Q1 | YoY |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | RM39.645m | RM18.305m | +116.6% |
| Gross profit | RM3.097m | RM2.289m | +35.3% |
| PBT | RM1.123m | RM0.752m | +49.3% |
| PAT | RM0.565m | RM0.638m | -11.4% |
| EPS | 0.32 sen | 0.36 sen | -11.1% |

### Margin signal
- Gross margin fell to ~7.8% from ~12.5%.
- PBT margin was ~2.8% vs ~4.1%.
- PAT margin was ~1.4% vs ~3.5%.
- The main issue is therefore not revenue demand, but profitability conversion.

## 3. Balance Sheet
- Total assets: RM103.35m vs RM109.34m at 28 Feb 2026.
- Inventories fell sharply to RM3.45m from RM10.38m.
- Contract assets increased to RM22.29m from RM18.49m.
- Trade receivables remained broadly flat at RM20.99m.
- Total liabilities increased to RM75.45m from RM68.90m.
- Total equity was RM33.89m.
- Borrowings: RM14.96m total (long + short term), plus RM1.04m bank overdraft.
- Reported cash was RM23.81m, but only RM6.87m remained after pledged deposits, earmarked balances and overdraft.

## 4. Cash Flow
Operating cash flow improved materially:
- FY27 Q1: +RM1.253m
- FY26 Q1: -RM2.615m

Working-capital movements were important:
- Inventory released RM6.922m of cash.
- Contract assets consumed RM3.619m.
- Trade receivables consumed RM1.349m.
- Contract liabilities contributed RM1.571m.
- Other payables contributed RM3.979m.

Investing cash flow was -RM0.207m and financing cash flow was -RM0.691m. Ending unrestricted/available cash-equivalent balance was RM6.869m.

## 5. Earnings Quality
Positive:
1. Revenue more than doubled.
2. PBT still grew 49%.
3. Operating cash flow turned positive.
4. Inventory reduction materially helped cash conversion.
5. Finance costs declined from RM264k to RM197k.

Caution:
1. PAT declined despite strong revenue growth.
2. Gross margin compressed significantly.
3. Tax expense rose to RM558k from RM114k, implying a much higher effective tax burden in the quarter.
4. Contract assets increased, so part of the revenue growth still needs to convert into cash.
5. Trade-receivable impairment charges of RM1.031m were recorded, partially offset by reversals.
6. Cash headline figure overstates immediately deployable liquidity because significant balances are pledged or earmarked.

## 6. Outlook — Next 2–4 Quarters
### Bull case
Revenue momentum continues, while project/product mix improves gross margin toward the prior-year level. Contract assets convert into receivables/cash and PAT begins to grow faster than revenue.

### Base case
Revenue remains strong, but gross margin stays around current levels. Earnings grow only modestly and cash conversion remains uneven.

### Bear case
High revenue growth is achieved through low-margin work, working capital remains tied up in contract assets, impairment/tax costs stay elevated, and PAT continues to lag revenue.

## 7. What I Will Monitor
1. **Gross margin:** most important KPI.
2. **PAT growth:** must eventually catch up with revenue growth.
3. **Contract assets:** whether RM22.3m converts to billing/cash.
4. **Trade receivables & impairment:** credit quality.
5. **Operating cash flow:** whether positive OCF becomes recurring.
6. **Borrowings/overdraft:** whether liquidity pressure rises.
7. **Next 2–3 quarters:** confirmation of whether Q1 revenue growth is sustainable.

### Bottom line
Cabnet is interesting because Q1 shows a major acceleration in revenue and a clear improvement in operating cash flow. However, the market should not treat +116.6% revenue growth as equivalent to earnings growth. The key investment thesis is now **“can Cabnet convert scale into higher-margin, higher-cash-flow earnings?”**
