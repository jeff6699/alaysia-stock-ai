# AGMO Holdings Berhad — Q1 FY2027 Financial Results

- **Company:** AGMO Holdings Berhad
- **Reporting period:** Quarter ended 30 June 2026 (Q1 FY2027)
- **Source:** Unaudited Interim Financial Report
- **Archived:** 2026-10-06
- **Source document:** Agmo Holdings Berhad Financial Results Q1 FY2027.pdf

## 1. Executive Summary

AGMO delivered **revenue growth but weaker profitability** in Q1 FY2027. Revenue rose 7.9% YoY to RM8.63m, while PBT fell 21.9% to RM0.98m and PAT fell 33.4% to RM0.69m. The main pressure came from Main Market transfer expenses, higher amortisation of development costs and higher selling/marketing expenses for JomeInvoice.

The more encouraging structural point is that **recurring revenue contribution increased from 26.7% to 29.4%**, while DocLab, an AI-powered bank statement analysis solution, contributed to digital platform growth.

Q1 FY2027 was also weaker sequentially: revenue fell 16.6% QoQ, PBT fell 63.4% and PAT fell 63.7%, mainly because of lower revenue from bespoke digital solutions.

## 3. Business Mix

- Bespoke digital solutions remained the largest contributor at **RM6.01m / 69.6% of group revenue**.
- Recurring revenue streams increased to **29.4%** of total revenue from 26.7% a year earlier.
- Digital platform-based services benefited from **DocLab**, described by the company as an AI-powered bank statement analysis solution.
- Application support and managed services remained a recurring-revenue component.
- Training/development revenue was small and declined YoY.

## 4. Balance Sheet & Cash

- Cash and bank/short-term investments: **RM25.09m**.
- Fixed deposits: **RM0.87m**.
- Total equity: **RM59.82m**.
- Total liabilities: **RM7.15m**.
- **No borrowings** as at 30 June 2026.
- Development costs rose to **RM13.23m** from RM11.90m.
- Operating cash flow was only **RM0.09m**, while RM1.69m of development costs were paid during the quarter.

The balance sheet remains strong, but the cash-conversion profile needs monitoring because accounting profit was not matched by operating cash flow in this quarter.

## 5. Management Outlook

Management plans to:
1. Expand enterprise software development and AI innovation offerings.
2. Grow recurring revenue through digital platforms and managed technology services.
3. Continue supporting customers' digital transformation.

The Board is **cautiously optimistic** about future prospects, citing an established customer base, proprietary technologies, strategic partnerships and an experienced engineering workforce. The company also acknowledges rapid technological change and evolving customer requirements as ongoing challenges.

## 6. Investment View — Source-based + Analysis

### Positive
- Revenue continues to grow.
- Gross margin improved materially.
- Recurring revenue mix is moving in the right direction.
- AI-related product exposure through DocLab.
- Strong balance sheet and no borrowings.
- Continued investment in R&D, sales/marketing and digital platforms.

### Watch / Negative
- Profit growth is currently weaker than revenue growth.
- PBT and PAT declined YoY.
- QoQ earnings dropped sharply.
- Operating cash flow was weak.
- Development costs are rising, increasing future amortisation requirements.
- JomeInvoice expansion is increasing selling/marketing costs before the revenue payoff is fully visible.
- Effective tax rate increased to 29.5% from 17.4%.
- Material litigation with Zitron remains pending; trial dates are scheduled for March and April 2027.

## 7. Forward-looking Assessment

**Overall outlook: Neutral-to-positive, but still in execution phase.**

The key investment thesis is **not Q1 earnings momentum**, but whether AGMO can successfully convert its AI/digital investments into higher recurring revenue and operating leverage.

### What would make the thesis stronger
- Recurring revenue continues rising above 30%.
- DocLab/JomeInvoice achieves meaningful commercial scaling.
- Revenue growth accelerates while operating expenses grow more slowly.
- PBT/PAT returns to growth.
- Operating cash flow begins tracking reported profit more closely.
- Development-cost investment produces measurable revenue and margin returns.

### What would weaken the thesis
- Revenue remains dependent on bespoke projects.
- Recurring revenue growth stalls.
- AI/platform investments continue consuming cash without sufficient monetisation.
- Profit margins continue contracting.
- Litigation creates a material financial or reputational impact.
