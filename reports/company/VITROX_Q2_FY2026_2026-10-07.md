# ViTrox Corporation Berhad (VITROX) — Q2 FY2026 Investment Research Summary

**Reporting period:** 30 June 2026  
**Source:** Unaudited Q2 FY2026 quarterly report  
**Archive date:** 7 October 2026

## 1. Key result

**Q2 FY2026 was a major earnings acceleration.**

- Revenue: RM374.9m, +104.8% YoY, +40.4% QoQ
- PBT: RM99.9m, +163.7% YoY, +62.1% QoQ
- PAT: RM84.5m, +203.9% YoY, +66.4% QoQ
- 1H revenue: RM642.0m, +98.0%
- 1H PAT attributable to owners: RM136.2m, +160.5%

Management attributes the growth primarily to AI infrastructure demand, automotive/consumer electronics, supply-chain restructuring, advanced packaging/HBM requirements, shipment acceleration and customer pull-ins.

## 2. Quality of the quarter

The earnings growth is stronger than the revenue growth, supported by manufacturing scale, product mix and operational optimisation. Q2 approximate PBT margin was 26.7% and PAT margin 22.5%.

The biggest counterpoint is cash conversion: 1H PAT was RM135.3m versus operating cash flow of only RM24.2m. Inventory and receivables absorbed RM276.7m of working capital combined.

## 3. Balance sheet

Cash RM369.0m versus borrowings RM82.2m implies approximate net cash of RM286.7m.

But inventory rose to RM429.3m and receivables to RM527.1m by 30 June 2026. These balances need to convert into cash without material deterioration.

## 4. Catalysts

- Sustained AI/data-centre infrastructure investment.
- HBM and advanced-packaging inspection demand.
- Higher ABI/MVS shipment volumes.
- Operating leverage from higher factory utilisation.
- VTSB tax exemption support.
- Longer-term opportunities from next-generation machine vision and AI Smart Factories.

## 5. Risks

- Semiconductor/electronics cyclicality.
- AI capex normalisation or customer pull-in reversal.
- Working-capital expansion and weak cash conversion.
- FX volatility.
- Geopolitical uncertainty.
- Margin normalisation after an unusually strong quarter.

## 6. Current investment stance from this report alone

**Fundamentals: STRONG POSITIVE**  
**Earnings momentum: STRONG POSITIVE**  
**Balance sheet: POSITIVE**  
**Cash-flow quality: WATCH**  
**Industry cycle: POSITIVE but cyclical**  
**Valuation: NOT ASSESSED**

This is a **high-quality earnings-growth update**, but not yet a complete Buy/Hold/Sell decision because valuation requires current market price and forward earnings assumptions.

## 7. Next decision checkpoint

The next quarter should be judged on three questions:

1. Does revenue continue to grow after the Q2 surge?
2. Does operating cash flow catch up with reported earnings?
3. Can margins remain structurally above the pre-AI-cycle level?

If all three remain positive, the Q2 result would support a stronger long-term earnings thesis. If revenue remains strong but receivables/inventory continue expanding faster than sales and cash flow stays weak, earnings quality risk increases.
