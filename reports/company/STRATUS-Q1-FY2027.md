# STRATUS GLOBAL HOLDINGS BERHAD — Q1 FY2027 Financial Results

> Source: Unaudited Interim Financial Report for the first quarter ended 30 June 2026.  
> Company: Stratus Global Holdings Berhad  
> Reporting period: Q1 FY2027 / quarter ended 30 June 2026  
> Note: This is the Group's first interim financial report after listing, so there are no corresponding Q1 FY2026 comparative figures.

## 1. Executive Summary

Stratus Global delivered a strong first reported quarter, with RM34.84 million revenue, RM17.90 million gross profit, RM12.46 million profit before tax and RM9.55 million profit after tax.

The headline strengths are:
- Gross margin: ~51.4%
- PBT margin: ~35.8%
- PAT margin: ~27.4%
- Operating cash flow: RM25.28 million
- Cash & cash equivalents: RM147.06 million
- Borrowings: Nil
- Net assets: RM238.03 million
- EPS: 0.76 sen

The balance sheet is particularly strong, with substantial cash and no borrowings. However, the business is project-based and revenue recognition depends on project completion, installation, commissioning and customer acceptance. Management explicitly cautions that quarterly results can fluctuate materially and should not be viewed in isolation.

Q1 revenue was geographically diversified:

- Europe: RM18.659m (~53.6%)

- North America: RM6.666m (~19.1%)

Europe and Malaysia together contributed approximately 80.86% of revenue.

## 4. Cash Flow & Balance Sheet

Operating cash flow was RM25.28m versus PAT of RM9.55m. The strong operating cash flow was supported mainly by working-capital movements, particularly a RM31.97m reduction in receivables.

At 30 June 2026:
- Cash increased from RM121.69m to RM147.06m.
- Receivables fell from RM77.20m to RM43.84m.
- Current liabilities fell from RM34.22m to RM16.81m.
- Net current assets increased to RM205.25m.
- Total equity increased to RM238.03m.
- There were no borrowings.

This gives Stratus Global considerable financial flexibility for expansion, although future quarters should be checked for the sustainability of cash generation.

## 5. Business & Industry Position

The Group is principally engaged in material handling systems and equipment and related automation services, substantially within a single operating segment.

The report states that global semiconductor sales grew at a 4.80% CAGR from 2022–2024, while the semiconductor automated material handling system (AMHS) industry expanded at a 7.42% CAGR over the same period.

Management identifies several structural growth drivers:
- Increasing global demand for electronics
- Rapid E&E technological advancement
- Generative AI driving demand for data centres, electronics and semiconductors
- Digitalisation investment and government initiatives
- Increasing semiconductor manufacturing automation
- Need to increase production capacity and throughput

## 7. Important Risk Flags

### A. Project-based earnings volatility
Revenue may only be recognised when projects reach relevant completion/acceptance milestones. A large order pipeline therefore does not automatically translate into current-period revenue or profit.

### B. Semiconductor cycle exposure
The business is linked to semiconductor capital expenditure and customer expansion plans. A slowdown in semiconductor capex could affect order timing and earnings.

### C. Customer/project timing
Quarterly results may fluctuate substantially because of installation, commissioning and customer acceptance timing.

### D. Geographic concentration
Europe and Malaysia accounted for ~80.86% of Q1 revenue. Future monitoring should assess whether geographic concentration decreases as overseas expansion progresses.

### E. Expansion execution
A large portion of IPO proceeds is earmarked for facility expansion, overseas offices and R&D. The key question is whether these investments generate sufficient incremental revenue and returns.

## 8. Investment Interpretation

### Positive
1. High gross margin for the first reported quarter.
2. Strong PAT margin.
3. Strong operating cash flow.
4. RM147m cash with zero borrowings.
5. Large IPO-funded expansion capacity.
6. Exposure to semiconductor automation and AI/data-centre-related semiconductor demand.
7. Management plans overseas expansion across Asia, Europe and the USA.

### Watch
1. No year-on-year comparison yet.
2. One quarter is insufficient to establish a durable earnings trend.
3. Project accounting can make quarterly earnings lumpy.
4. Need to see whether margins remain above 45–50% as revenue scales.
5. Need to monitor order book/order intake, customer concentration and cash conversion.
6. Need to evaluate returns generated from the RM285m IPO proceeds.

## 11. Bottom Line

**Stratus Global's Q1 FY2027 report is fundamentally strong, especially on margins, cash position and balance-sheet strength.**

The investment thesis is attractive because the company sits in semiconductor automation/AMHS, an industry that management expects to benefit from semiconductor growth, automation and generative-AI-related demand.

However, this should currently be classified as **"promising but still needs validation"**, rather than a proven compounder. The next 2–4 quarters are critical for confirming whether Q1 profitability and cash generation are sustainable and whether the IPO-funded expansion can translate into higher earnings.
