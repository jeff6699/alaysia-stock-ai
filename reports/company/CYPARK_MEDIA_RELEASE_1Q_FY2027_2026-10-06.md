# CYPARK Resources Berhad — 1Q FY2027 Media Release Summary

> Source: Cypark media release dated 22 September 2026  
> Period: First quarter ended 31 July 2026  
> Archive date: 2026-10-06  
> Source basis: Uploaded media release only

## 1. Executive Summary

Cypark delivered a strong earnings turnaround in 1QFY2027, returning to profitability. Revenue more than quadrupled year-on-year to RM180.0 million, while PBT turned positive at RM5.7 million from an LBT of RM17.6 million in 1QFY2026. PAT also turned positive at RM5.4 million from a net loss of RM18.1 million.

The company highlighted that the turnaround was driven by underlying operations rather than a one-off accounting item.

## 2. Key Financial Highlights

| Metric | 1QFY2027 | 1QFY2026 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | RM180.0m | RM41.6m | +332.7% YoY |
| Gross Profit | RM29.2m | -RM0.3m | Turned positive |
| PBT | RM5.7m | -RM17.6m | Turned positive |
| PAT | RM5.4m | -RM18.1m | Turned positive |

1QFY2027 revenue also exceeded the RM179.2 million recorded for the whole of FY2026.

## 3. Sequential Turnaround

Revenue increased from RM48.1 million in 4QFY2026 to RM180.0 million in 1QFY2027, nearly fourfold QoQ.

Excluding the RM39.0 million one-off non-cash impairment reversal recorded in 4QFY2026, underlying earnings improved from an LBT of RM21.9 million in 4QFY2026 to PBT of RM5.7 million in 1QFY2027, a RM27.6 million sequential improvement.

## 4. Business Segment Drivers

### Renewable Energy (RE)
External revenue increased 4.9% YoY to RM29.8 million.

### Circular Waste & Waste-to-Energy (WtE)
Revenue increased 36.2% YoY to RM17.7 million, supported by higher electricity generation, higher waste volume and the full-quarter impact of revised tipping fees.

### Sustainable Engineering & Infrastructure (SE&I)
SE&I emerged as the major new earnings engine, generating RM132.5 million revenue and RM21.9 million EBITDA as project execution gathered momentum.

The combination of recurring RE and WtE operations with a scalable engineering and infrastructure platform is creating a more diversified earnings base.

## 5. Orderbook and Growth Visibility

Cypark reported:
- Orderbook: approximately RM2.4 billion
- Active tenderbook: approximately RM2.2 billion

The company sees expanding opportunities across:
- Renewable energy
- Energy storage
- Sustainable infrastructure
- Waste management

The next phase is focused on converting the existing orderbook into revenue and earnings.

## 7. Outlook

**Source-based outlook: Positive, but execution-dependent.**

Cypark management views 1QFY2027 as an important turning point and expects FY2027 and beyond to benefit from stronger execution, orderbook conversion and growth opportunities across its core markets.

From an investment-research perspective, the key transition to monitor is:

**Orderbook → Project Execution → Revenue → EBITDA → PBT/PAT → Cash Flow**

If this chain continues to strengthen over the next several quarters, the earnings turnaround could develop into a broader growth phase. However, this media release alone does not establish that the turnaround is already durable.
