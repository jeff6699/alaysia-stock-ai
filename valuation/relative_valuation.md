# Relative and Cash-Flow Valuation Methods

## Shared evidence rules
Use dated prices, comparable periods, diluted shares, consistent currency and defensible peers. FACT = reported/observed inputs; ANALYSIS = normalization/method; ASSUMPTION = selected earnings, growth, multiple, yield and adjustments; INFERENCE = qualified conclusion; FORECAST = forward values from assumptions. Cite sources and explain N/A inputs. Peer medians are references, not intrinsic values.

## P/E
P/E = dated share price / diluted EPS (trailing or forward). Fair value/share = normalized diluted EPS × justified P/E.
FACT: price date, reported EPS/shares and peer/history. ASSUMPTION: normalized EPS and multiple based on growth, returns, quality, leverage, cyclicality, peer/history or DCF. N/A for negative/unstable/nonrepresentative EPS or missing data. Do not use peak-cycle EPS without normalization or label forecast EPS as FACT.

## EV/EBITDA
EV = equity market cap + debt + included lease liabilities + preferred claims + NCI − excess cash/non-operating assets.
Implied EV = normalized EBITDA × justified EV/EBITDA.
Equity = implied EV − debt/leases/claims + excess cash/non-operating assets.
Fair value/share = equity / diluted shares.
ASSUMPTION: normalized EBITDA, lease basis, peer/history multiple and adjustments. Usually N/A for banks/insurers or where EBITDA hides capex/working capital.

## PEG
Observed PEG = P/E / expected annual EPS growth in percentage points (18x / 12% = 1.5).
If target PEG is justified: fair P/E = target PEG × sustainable EPS growth percentage points; fair value/share = normalized diluted EPS × fair P/E.
Growth and target PEG are ASSUMPTIONS/FORECASTS with horizon/evidence. Never default to PEG=1. N/A for negative/near-zero/volatile EPS or unsupported growth; cross-check only.

## P/B
P/B = equity market value / owners’ equity or price/share / book value/share on a matched basis.
Fair value/share = sustainable BVPS × justified P/B.
For stable financials, cross-check justified P/B = (sustainable ROE − g)/(cost of equity − g), only if cost of equity > g and steady-state assumptions hold.
ASSUMPTION: sustainable ROE, growth, cost of equity, asset quality and multiple. N/A for negative equity or unreliable book.

## Dividend yield / DDM
Observed yield = trailing cash DPS / dated price; this is not intrinsic value.
Stable-yield implied fair price = sustainable annual DPS / required dividend yield.
Gordon DDM = next expected DPS/(cost of equity − sustainable dividend growth), with cost of equity > growth and sustainable payout. Multi-stage DDM = PV of explicit dividends plus discounted terminal distribution.
ASSUMPTION: DPS/payout, growth, required yield/cost of equity and special-dividend treatment. FACT distinguishes proposed, declared, approved and paid. N/A if no stable payout or funding is weak; do not capitalize a special dividend forever.

## FCF yield
Pair cash flow with denominator:
- FCFF yield = normalized FCFF / EV; compare with WACC.
- FCFE yield = normalized FCFE / equity market cap; compare with cost of equity.
Never mix FCFF with market cap or FCFE with EV.
Implied EV = FCFF / required FCFF yield; implied equity = FCFE / required FCFE yield. Divide equity by diluted shares after explicit bridge.
ASSUMPTION: normalized FCF and required yield. N/A for unstable/negative cash flow or unsuitable sector.

## SOTP
Value separately disclosed segments with suitable methods and distinct FACTS/ASSUMPTIONS. Sum EVs or equity values consistently; reconcile debt, NCI, central costs/claims once; add non-operating assets once; divide by diluted shares. Holding discounts require evidence.
Useful for distinct supportable segments; N/A for weak disclosure. Avoid double-counting subsidiaries, associates, cash, debt or intersegment sales.

## Blend
Blend only comparable per-share equity values. Never mix EV/equity, multiples, yields or N/A. Explain weights (ASSUMPTIONS), correlations and method limits; included weights sum to 100%. Investigate divergences rather than forcing convergence.
